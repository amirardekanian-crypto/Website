# WHATSAPP — what Amir sends when the ad works

Every ad that asks for WhatsApp ships with a first reply (`SKILL.md` step 3). This file is the
standing set those replies are cut from.

**Why it exists.** Amir answers every message personally with no template (BRAND §8). That is good
for trust and it is the leakiest point in the system: an ad spends reach to produce one message, and
a slow or vague reply wastes it. These are not scripts to paste blindly — they are his own argument,
written down, so a reply takes thirty seconds instead of ten minutes.

⚠️ **The Farsi below is my draft, not his voice yet.** Amir should rewrite the phrasing once and
then it is his. The *structure* is the part to keep.

---

## The rules

1. **Speed beats polish.** A rough reply in ten minutes converts better than a perfect one tomorrow.
   If he cannot answer properly, send T0 and come back.
2. **Qualify before pitching.** T1 asks two questions. Someone who answers them is a buyer; someone
   who does not was never going to be.
3. **The ad's last line and T1 carry the same promise, word for word.** If the ad said
   «هفته‌ی ۱ رو رایگان امتحان کن», T1 opens on the demo, not on the price.
4. **Never discount, never chase.** One follow-up at most (T9). The price is the price — «یه‌بار
   پرداخت، بدون تاریخ انقضا» is the argument, not a discount.
5. **Never diagnose over WhatsApp.** Pain goes to T8 and to a doctor. The course says plainly
   «این دوره پزشک شما نیست»; so does he.
6. **Say no when it is a no** (T8). A refused sale that was a bad fit costs one sale; a bad fit who
   buys costs a refund, a bad review and a story told at the club.
7. **Route to coaching when that is the honest answer** (T6). The course is the affordable,
   self-guided version of the same method — someone who wants to be watched should be told the
   coaching exists.

## What the product actually is — Amir's own words (2026-09-21)

Use these as the source for any "what is it" line, in any ad or reply:

- **It is the cheaper way to get his method** than the personal coaching he sells.
- **Structured progression** — it builds on itself rather than being a pile of workouts.
- **Education, not just prescription** — how to perform each movement, plus the lessons.
- **Everything a tennis player needs, in one place.**
- **All three environments: home, gym and on court.**
- **It develops every physical quality a tennis player needs, based on science.**

⚠️ **Never say "cheap version".** It is the same method, self-guided, at a price anyone can do. The
difference is who does the thinking week to week, not how good the training is.

### T3 · "قیمت؟" — the most common first message

⚠️ **Most people open with the price, before any value has been established** (Amir, 2026-09-21).
He thinks $17 is very cheap; they hear it as expensive. That gap is not closed by listing more
features — it is closed by **changing the unit they are comparing**.

**Never dodge the number.** Answering "let me tell you what's inside first" reads as a sales move
and costs trust. Give the number in the first line, and anchor it in the second.

> معادلِ ۱۷ دلار به تومان، یه‌بار. بدونِ تاریخِ انقضا.
>
> یه مقایسه که شاید کمک کنه: برنامه‌ی اختصاصیِ من ماهی معادلِ ۲۵ دلاره. این دوره ۱۶ هفته‌ست —
> یعنی همون مدت، اختصاصی، می‌شه حدودِ ۱۰۰ دلار. این ۱۷ دلاره و برای همیشه مالِ خودته.
>
> داخلش: ۱۶ هفته برنامه‌ی کامل — باشگاه، زمین و خونه — ۱۱۴ حرکت با نکته‌ی اجرا، ۲۲ درس و ۷ تست.
> با سرعتِ خودت جلو می‌ری.
>
> اگه بخوای شروع کنی:
> ۱. مبلغ رو کارت‌به‌کارت می‌کنی
> ۲. رسید رو همین‌جا می‌فرستی
> ۳. نام کاربری و رمزت رو برات می‌فرستم

⚠️ **Fill the card details in his phone's saved replies, never in this repo.**

### T3b · «گرونه» — when they say it is expensive

The move is the **unit**, not the discount. Four numbers, all checkable on his own site.

> می‌فهمم. بذار جورِ دیگه‌ای حسابش کنیم.
>
> ۱۶ هفته‌ست، یعنی حدودِ ۴ ماه. می‌شه ماهی حدودِ ۴ دلار. و بعدِ اون هم تموم نمی‌شه — هر وقت خواستی
> دوباره از اولش اجراش کن، بدونِ پرداختِ دوباره.
>
> همون ۴ ماه، برنامه‌ی اختصاصی، می‌شه حدودِ ۱۰۰ دلار. فرقش اینه که اونجا من هر هفته برنامه رو از
> روی خودت می‌نویسم و داده‌هات رو می‌بینم. اینجا همون روشه، ولی خودت اجراش می‌کنی.
>
> و اگه هنوز مطمئن نیستی، اصلاً پول نده — اول رایگان ببینش. هفته‌ی ۱ کامل، یه تست و ۳ درس بازه،
> بدونِ ثبت‌نام: amirardekani.com/tennis/app/?demo=1

**The four anchors, in the order they work:**

| Anchor | The line | Why it lands |
|---|---|---|
| **Same 16 weeks** | ۱۰۰ دلار اختصاصی در برابرِ ۱۷ دلار | Compares like with like. The strongest one — lead with it. |
| **Per month** | ماهی حدودِ ۴ دلار | Turns one lump into a small recurring number that is not recurring. |
| **No expiry** | همیشه مالِ خودته، دوباره هم می‌تونی اجراش کنی | The only product he sells that is not rented. |
| **Their own speed** | با سرعتِ خودت | Removes the fear of paying and falling behind. |

⚠️ **Never discount, never invent a comparison.** A private session with a trainer probably costs
more than the whole course — that would be the sharpest anchor of all, but **only Amir knows the
real local number**. Do not write it until he supplies it, and then write it as a fact, not a guess.

⚠️ **Numbers to keep true.** $17 once · $25/month for the personal programme · 16 weeks ≈ 4 months ·
so ≈$100 for the same period. All of these are on `index-fa.html` and `/tennis/`. **If Amir raises
the Iran coaching price, this file, the Farsi homepage and `Content/PRODUCT.md` all move together**
— otherwise the anchor contradicts his own public page and a customer will catch it.

### T8 · When it is a no

> داره بدتر می‌شه، اول پزشک یا فیزیوتراپ. این دوره پزشکت نیست و نمی‌خوام چیزی رو بدتر کنم.

**Padel player:**
> این دوره برای تنیس نوشته شده. برای پدل، برنامه‌ی اختصاصی بگیر — بگو تا توضیح بدم.

## Routing

```
message arrives
 ├─ "قیمت؟" (the usual opener)          → T3 → T3b if "گرونه" → T4
 └─ anything else → T1 (two questions)
     ├─ trains already, clear problem     → T2 → T3 → T4
     ├─ "it's free online"                → T5 → T3
     ├─ "I want someone watching me"      → T6 → coaching, or T3
     ├─ "I'll think about it"             → T7 → (48h) T9 → stop
     └─ hurt / too new / padel            → T8
```
