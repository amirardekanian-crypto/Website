The Coaching Graph

Your apps already collect most of what a good coach knows about an athlete. The problem is that it's stored in separate places that don't connect. These 13 ideas connect them, so the app can show an athlete why each thing is in their plan, what it trains, and how it's working. Four more grew out of building them.

Built so far: #1 The Spine · #4 Because · #5 The Card Remembers, plus the History Sheet, Rungs and Who Has This. Next up: #16 Not Yet.
