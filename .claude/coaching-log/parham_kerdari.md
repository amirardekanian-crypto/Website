## Cycle 01 — Foundation Forge · 2026-08-21 · NEW

**The read**

*Recovery ceiling (assessed first, it gates everything).* Sleep 5–6 h at 5/10, stress 6/10,
"long days, little downtime", and he is a **concurrent athlete** — 3–4 padel sessions a week
that are high-CNS and full of hard decelerations, none of it monitored. He arrives **detrained**
(0 current training days). Ceiling set at **~8–12 hard sets per priority muscle/week**, bottom of
the evidence range, max ~6 working exercises per session, plyo contacts capped low. Distribution
is the lever, not total volume.

*The diagnosis.* His own words: fatigue resistance is good but errors climb through a match, and
he is strong but feels slow. Those are one problem — **rate of force development and reactivity**,
not max strength, not aerobic capacity. Years of powerlifting on a 94 kg frame is exactly the
profile. The all-day lethargy he dates to his weight cut is, on the intake numbers, far more
likely sleep + life load than fuelling (Amir confirmed the cut is finished and he is eating at
maintenance).

*Training age — the one real conflict.* Form says 1–3 years gym / 0 current days / no best lifts;
he told Amir years of powerlifting. **Amir's call: take the form at face value**, conservative
loading. Direction is unchanged (the complaint is slowness, not weakness) — "conservative" means
we never prescribe off a claimed max we have not seen. Hence the RPE 7 ceiling and the film gate.

*Contraindications.* No injuries, no history, nothing to avoid. Real risks are behavioural:
he will load his first squat like the man he used to be; 5–6 h sleep caps CNS work; and the
double-day rule is mandatory because most gym days will also be padel days.

*Structure.* Roadmap C1 asks for lifting tolerance + real baselines + first elastic/rotational
exposure. Full-body ×3 gets every pattern twice a week, which is what a detrained athlete needs.

**Decisions**

```
PRIMARY LIFT SELECTIONS
  Knee-dominant  → Barbell Back Squat    transfer > SFR; he owns the pattern; trackable baseline; no back history to defer axial load for
  Hinge          → Trap Bar Deadlift     force into the floor; kinder spinal position while detrained; direct ancestor of C3 power work
  Upper push     → Dumbbell Bench Press  ONE slot only — overhead sport, horizontal press volume deliberately low (tennis/posture rule)
  Upper pull     → Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row (D1) + Lat Pulldown (D2)   2:1 pull:push
  Rotational     → Medicine Ball Rotational Throw   the "قدرت برای ضربه" ask, near-free in fatigue
  Elastic        → Pogo Jump (D1) · Lateral Line Hop (D3), low amplitude only — no depth jumps at 94 kg
  Groin          → Copenhagen Plank, short lever
```

*Forks Amir settled at the checkpoint:*
- **Third day = a FULL lifting session**, overriding the recommended short ~30-min speed/tissue
  day. Recorded as his call. Cost: weekly total now sits at the *top* of the ceiling above rather
  than inside it — cut-trigger written into the handoff.
- **Back Squat kept** as C1 primary with the RPE 7 ceiling (his Faraz leg-press precedent was
  considered and set aside: that athlete had a back history, this one does not).
- **Film gate approved** on Back Squat + Trap Bar, weeks 1–2.
- **No conditioning in C1** — his engine is his strongest quality and padel already supplies it.

*Panel.* Roadmap ran the 3-lens + judge shape (speed/power · racket-sport transfer ·
recovery-constrained). Judge's two binding flags carried into this design: C2 must not become a
powerlifting block, and the double-day rule is mandatory. Judge argued for four cycles — overruled
per the 5×5 house rule; C5 (repeat-effort power) does real work because late-match decay is his
actual complaint. Design draft then went through the adversarial panel (clinical · house-rules
lint · dose/time). Clinical kept the D1 Pogo + Calf Raise pairing after review and flagged the
week-1 DOMS risk of a 3s-eccentric squat against 3–4 padel days (answered by the mandatory
calibration note, not by changing the card). Lint fixed three canonical names and moved
Copenhagen + Side Plank to `Tempo Iso`. Dose audit landed the days at 54.5 / 54.8 / 56 min.
