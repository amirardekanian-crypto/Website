## Cycle 01 — Armour Build · 2026-08-22 · NEW

**The read**

*Recovery ceiling — set before the session count.* Sleep 5-6 h at 4/10, stress 7/10, heavy life load,
inconsistent nutrition, **and 5-6 padel days a week**. The gym is 135 minutes sitting on top of ~500
court minutes. Ceiling: ~37 hard sets/week total. The clock is the binding constraint, not the
exercise list.

*Structure.* 3 days: Upper pull → Lower → Upper press. Full-body ×3 was the obvious alternative and
loses — it spends a third of a tiny budget on legs padel already floods 5-6 days a week, and drops
upper-body frequency, which is the half padel pays nothing for and where a 68 kg male's "shape"
lives. 2:1 upper:lower.

*Contraindications.* Two disc levels, both quiet, so this is a loading case not a rehab case. Nothing
sits on his shoulders, nothing goes overhead, no loaded spinal flexion, no free hinge until the brace
is built. Deliberately NOT cotton wool: he trains to RPE 8 on everything he does own.

*The neck is the product, not a side-project.* Chin tucks in every warm-up (deep neck flexors want
frequency, not load) plus one graded hold running first on Day 1, unfatigued, which doubles as the
weekly symptom check. Plus a daily hand-resisted hold at home so a missed gym day is not a zero week.

*Padel is itself an overhead sport* — bandejas, viboras, smashes, tracking lobs put his neck into
extension 5-6 days a week whatever the gym does. So the gym's job is to make that position tolerable
(scapular upward rotation, thoracic extension, isometric holds in neutral), not to pretend the
exposure is avoidable. This is the read that shaped the whole neck thread.

*Behavioural / expectation.* He is 68 kg on inconsistent food and 5-6 h sleep. He can execute this
perfectly and gain very little. Nutrition and sleep are levers in C1, not deferred to month four —
two habits only (protein at every meal 5/7, +30 min sleep 4 nights), tracked in Proof.

**Decisions — PRIMARY LIFT SELECTIONS**
- Horizontal pull → **Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row** (zero spinal load, best mid-back builder, high SFR). **Cycle benchmark: e1RM up by close.**
- Vertical pull → **Lat Pulldown**, neutral grip
- Knee-dominant → **Machine Leg Press** (axial deferred; film-gated depth cap). Explicitly a *baseline anchor, not a progress measure* — wk 1 is RPE-capped, wk 5 backs off, and wk 2 is his first ever leg press, so an e1RM read would measure load-finding.
- Trunk-supported hinge → **45° Back Extension** + **Machine Hip Thrust** (house pair)
- Press → **Incline Dumbbell Press**, volume deliberately low
- Overhead substitute → **Half-Kneeling Single-Arm Landmine Press**

**recovery ceiling**, not court exposure alone. Do not copy the weaker justification forward to C2.

**Per-day load** — cost-weighted 13.5 / 16.0 / 12.0 (33% spread). Sessions ~47 / ~50 / ~43 min.
Flat weeks are the named failure mode for a poor-recovery athlete; the first draft came in at a 7%
spread and was corrected.

**Progression levers**
- *Chest-Supported DB Row* — hit 10 at ≤RPE 8 → small jump. Ceiling RPE or missed reps → repeat.
- *Machine Leg Press* — **film-gated**. 10 reps at ≤RPE 8 *with the depth cap held on film* → load moves. No film, no jump.
- *45° Back Extension* — bodyweight wks 1-2; load only once 12 reps finish in a straight line with zero spinal movement.
- *Hand-Resisted Neck Hold* — hold duration before load, ever.
- Week 1 RPE-capped at 6 (recalibration), week 5 back-off to RPE 6, floor stated in both.

**e1RM** — none. New athlete, no logged sets. Back Squat 70 kg × 12 on record (≈98 kg Epley) is a
*pre-layoff self-report on a lift this cycle does not program*. Not a baseline; do not treat it as one.
