## Cycle 01 — Groundwork · 2026-08-17 · NEW

**The read** — 31M, 90 kg, carrying extra weight, under a year in the gym, currently
training 1-2 days. Plays tennis 3-4 days. Form says Semi-Pro; Amir's read is "he is not
that athletic," so the whole cycle is programmed to demonstrated capacity, not the label.
No injuries, no restrictions, no movements to avoid — every risk here is load management,
not pathology.

Recovery capacity is the binding constraint and it is worse than it looks: the gym is only
~40% of his training week, so tennis contributes lower-body eccentric and CNS load I never
see in a log. Sleep 6/10 (7-8h), stress 6/10, nutrition inconsistent, life load moderate.
Volume ceiling set at ~8-12 hard sets/week for priority muscles — the FLOOR of the 10-20
evidence range, deliberately: sub-1-year training age adapts to 10 as well as 20, tennis
is adding unlogged volume, and he is stepping up from 1-2 gym days to 3. Adherence is the
currency, and the whole 10→20 runway stays free for Cycle 2.

Structure: 3x full body over Push/Pull/Legs. PPL loses on two counts — it gives each
muscle one exposure per week (wrong for a trainee whose bottleneck is motor learning), and
a missed session costs an entire muscle group rather than a fraction. Full body buys 2-3
exposures per pattern and builds adherence insurance into the structure itself.

Selection is SFR-led with sport transfer deferred to C3/C4 per the roadmap and the
accumulation-block principle. No baseline lifts on file, no spotter, no coach in the room,
90 kg on a beginner frame — dumbbells and machines over barbell bench and back squat is
the only honest read. Goblet Squat's load ceiling is a feature: when it caps out, that is
the signal he has earned the barbell in C2. No plyometrics, no jumping, no depth work
anywhere this cycle — 90 kg plus <1yr training plus 6-7 training days is a tendon-loading
problem, and Steel Brakes (C3) is where that gets earned.

**Decisions** —

```
PRIMARY LIFT SELECTIONS
  Squat pattern    → Goblet Squat (pattern teaching; self-limiting load = graduation test)
  Loaded quad      → Machine Leg Press (SFR; supplies load the goblet can't)
  Hinge            → Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift (highest-value tennis pattern; dowel teaches it in prep)
  Horizontal pull  → Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row (SFR; spares low back for hinge + court)
  Vertical pull    → Lat Pulldown (pull-ups impossible at his bodyweight/training age)
  Horizontal push  → Dumbbell Bench Press (teaches more than machine; safe with no spotter)
  Single-leg       → Dumbbell Reverse Lunge (sport-relevant; gentlest unilateral entry)
  Vertical push    → Machine Shoulder Press (SFR; low shoulder cost alongside serving)
```

Forks Amir settled at the checkpoint:

1. **Body-comp lever** → nutrition note + ONE rowing finisher on the low day. Rejected
   adding conditioning across multiple days: he already has 3-4 court days, and his
   inconsistent food is worth more kilos than any circuit.
2. **Press volume** → Amir overrode the "tennis clients get low horizontal press" default
   and called for 9+ sets, on the grounds that his stated goal names physique and he has no
   shoulder or posture history. Landed at 10 chest sets/week (DB Bench 4 + Cable Chest Fly
   3 + Machine Incline Chest Press 3), rebalanced across all three days. Back held at 9 so
   the pull:push ratio stays honest for an overhead athlete.

**Progression levers** —

- **Goblet Squat** — lever: load. Add-trigger: 10 clean reps at RPE ≤7 across all 4 sets.
  Increment: next bell up. Deload call: if RPE ≥9 two sessions running, hold and repeat.
  **GRADUATION:** when the heaviest available bell is easy, he has earned the barbell in C2.
- **Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift** — lever: load, **gated on FILM**. Weight moves only after
  Amir has seen a top set and cleared the back position. Increment: small. This is the

- **Dumbbell Bench Press** — lever: load. Add-trigger: 10 reps at RPE ≤7 both hands.
- **Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row / Lat Pulldown** — lever: load, then reps to the top of range.
- **Dumbbell Reverse Lunge** — lever: bodyweight → dumbbells → load. Gate is balance, not strength.
