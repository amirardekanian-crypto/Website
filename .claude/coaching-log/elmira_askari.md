## Cycle 02 — Growth Engine · 2026-07-27 · RETURNING

**The read** — *(first logged entry; C1 reconstructed from the shipped file + 10 session reports)*

**Adherence is the headline, not programming.** C1 ran 4 days × 5 weeks (Jun 6 – Jul 10) and

### Movement-variety correction · 2026-07-27 (Amir)

Amir caught the actual mechanism behind the last fix's 5-set lateral raises: **padding sets onto
an existing movement instead of adding a new one is itself a corner-cut**, functionally the same
failure as duplicating an exercise across days — reaching a number without doing the design work
of finding fresh stimulus. He also flagged **zero chest exercises** as wrong for an intermediate
athlete on a physique cycle (the posture principle argues for *low* horizontal-press volume, not
*none*), and asked for **Cable Face Pull back** (removed two commits ago to make Day 4 fit, but
Day 4 is now pure lower-body, so it has to live on an upper day instead).

**Amir also clarified scope: exercise selection is not limited to `exercise_library.json`.**
That file is the video-join key, not a whitelist — I can prescribe any real movement that fits the
athlete, following only the naming *convention*, not restricted to what's already catalogued. A
new exercise simply ships without a demo video until it's filmed and added to Notion; the app
handles a missing video gracefully (no play button, nothing breaks). Recorded as a durable fact:
**do not treat the library as a hard boundary on programming decisions going forward.**

Fix — both padded exercises revert to a normal dose, and each is paired with one fresh movement
rather than a second lateral-raise variant:

- **D1** — Cable Lateral Raise 5→**4 sets**. Seated Single-Arm Cable Row removed (back was
  already covered by Lat Pulldown + Chest-Supported Row; dropping it frees room without breaching
  the back floor). **Cable Face Pull re-added** (3×20 @8) and **Machine Chest Press added** —
  first chest exercise this cycle, dosed light and framed in its own note as support, not focus.
- **D3** — Dumbbell Lateral Raise 5→**4 sets**. Incline Y-T-W Raise removed (Machine Reverse Fly
  already covers the same rear-delt purpose; two isolation rear-delt moves on one day was
  redundant). **Leaning Cable Lateral Raise added** — a cross-body cable raise, leaning away from
  the stack, chosen specifically because it keeps tension on the side delt through the top of the
  range where a standard raise goes slack. Not yet in `exercise_library.json`; ships with a setup
  note since the athlete hasn't seen this variant before and there's no demo video yet.

Both days land at exactly 7 working exercises — the accepted ceiling, not over it. Volume:
side delts hold at **15 (11 direct)**, rear delts hold at **12 (10 direct)** — the fix moved
*where* the sets come from, not how many there are. Back drops 11→**8**, exactly at its floor.
Chest goes 0→**3**, developing. Per-day time **58 / 57 / 59 / 51 min** — tighter than before this
fix, not longer.

RPE across five weeks IS the win**; chasing load here is how you lose the muscle the cycle exists
to protect.

**Training age: Amir's correction, and it drove the final shape.** Mid-design Amir called her
**"a beginner and very weak"** and removed the supersets the draft had built Day 3 around. That
also removed the density lever, so this cycle progresses on load and reps only, and every session
runs straight sets with independent rest.

**Structure: 3 days at the athlete's request (time).** The shape is forced, not chosen. The
women's ≥10 sets/week floor on quads, hams and glutes is 30 lower-body sets. One lower day
breaches the ~10-direct-sets-per-session ceiling; two lower days leave a single upper day that
cannot carry the shoulder and back work inside 7 exercises. The only structure satisfying both is
**D1 Upper · D2 Lower · D3 Full body**. D1 goes first because it is her priority and historically
her most-attended day. Amir confirmed her constraint is **trips to the gym, not total time**.

**⚖️ POSTURE became the organising idea (Amir, 2026-09-05).** *"She wants a flat back as she has
rounded shoulder and forward head posture — maybe add more to upper back or add some mobility."*
This is the change that gives the cycle its identity. Rounded shoulders are not fixed by stretching
the chest; they are fixed by making the scapular retractors and lower traps strong enough to hold
position unconsciously. So:
- **Back went to 15 sets** — Lat Pulldown, Cable Row, Chest-Supported DB Row, Prone Y-T-W.
- **Prone Y-T-W and Cable Face Pull** are the two doing the real postural work, both dosed light
  with an explicit "if you feel it in your neck, the weight is wrong" note.
- **Thoracic and cervical mobility moved into prep, where it costs no set budget** — Chin Tuck
  (forward head) and Open Book (thoracic rotation) on D1, Open Book again on D3.
- **Chest deliberately stays at 3.** This is the one place the "every major group 10-20" rule is
  NOT followed, and it is deliberate: loading horizontal pressing on a rounded-shoulder,
  forward-head client works against the exact thing the cycle exists to fix, and Amir's own
  standing principle says posture clients get horizontal-press volume low or omitted. Flagged to
  him rather than silently resolved either way.

**⚖️ The shoulder-as-one-muscle rule (Amir, 2026-09-05), which landed in two steps.**
First: *"The whole shoulder total load should be 10 sets total. So if she is doing rear delt, it
counts."* The draft had treated side, rear and front delts as three separate muscles each with its
own range, inflating shoulders to 26 sets/week. Then, when the rewrite landed at exactly 10, Amir
corrected the correction: *"10 is not a cap, 10-20 is good."*

So the settled reading is **the shoulder is ONE muscle group, scored on the normal 10-20 range**.
This cycle carries **14** — Machine Shoulder Press 4, Cable Lateral Raise 3, Machine Lateral Raise 4,
Cable Face Pull 3. Per the 4-set rule the extra volume arrived as a **new exercise**, not as sets on
an existing one, and Machine Lateral Raise was chosen specifically because a machine stack starts
lighter than a cable pin — the exact reason she came off the cable raise in C2.

Note the boundary this draws: **rear delt counts as shoulder, scapular retractors count as back**.
That is what lets the posture volume sit at 15 without being double-counted against the shoulder
total.

**⚖️ The 4-set rule (Amir, 2026-09-05).** *"For an intermediate athlete, never go for more than 4
sets in an exercise. Always add a new exercise."* Caught Machine Lying Leg Curl at 6 sets — the
only breach in the cycle, and the same corner-cut as the 2026-07-27 movement-variety ruling. Fixed
the way the rule prescribes: **Lying Leg Curl 6 → 4, and Machine Seated Leg Curl added at 3** (a
genuinely distinct hip-flexed angle she has never done). Hamstrings went 10 → 11 in the process.

**The thing that actually decides this cycle is not programming.** Body weight has not moved in two
cycles, and it was never going to — C1 taught patterns and C2 built muscle. The fat-loss lever is
NEAT, and **her step data is a logging artifact, not a measurement**: across 42 days every value
was 0, 500, 1000, 2000 or 3000, all multiples of the app's tap increment, with not one organic
number. She is tapping the counter once, not walking 500 steps. The notes card asks for a real
number *before* setting any target. Body weight has exactly one reading (70 kg, 2026-09-05).

**Injury / movement.** No musculoskeletal injury. **One standing watch item, not closed:**

written against the ovarian-cyst context (CA 125 6.2). C3 keeps the hip thrust and adds a cable
hinge, end-range abduction and a V-Up — all in that category — so the card carries forward,

the clinical audit lens caught that.

**Roadmap.** No deviation. C3 is Metabolic Shift, 5 weeks, weeks 12-16 (2026-09-08 → 2026-10-12).
