## Cycle 02 — Growth Engine · 2026-07-27 · RETURNING

**The read** — *(first logged entry; C1 reconstructed from the shipped file + 10 session reports)*

**Adherence is the headline, not programming.** C1 ran 4 days × 5 weeks (Jun 6 – Jul 10) and
delivered **9 of 20 planned sessions (45%)**, then a 26-day gap and one session back on Jul 25.
Distribution was the real problem: **D1 ×5, D2 ×3, D3 ×1, D4 ×1** — she reliably trains the
first one or two days of the week and the back half of the split effectively never happened.
Since C1 parked the rear delts, single-leg and side-glute work on D3/D4, the posture and
"shape" goals were the exact things not getting trained.

**The volume audit makes it concrete.** C1 was billed as shoulders + glutes and prescribed
**26 glute sets/week against 6 direct side-delt sets**. Adjusted for what she actually did,
side delts got **~2.6 sets/week over seven weeks**. Amir's "work more on her shoulders" is
what the data already said.

**Adaptation.** One genuine win: Barbell Hip Thrust 10→20→30 kg × 10 at a flat RPE 8. Lat
Pulldown quietly improved (6×10@8 → 6×12@8). Everything else stalled or ran hot — RDL pinned
at 20 kg @9–10, Machine Shoulder Press to RPE 10, Cable Lateral Raise 10/10/10 vs prescribed 9,
Bulgarian 9/9/10 with rep drop-off. Hack Squat, Face Pull and Hip Abduction never moved *and*
ran **below** target RPE — a missing progression rule, not missing capacity.

**Recovery & lifestyle.** Sleep is the limiter (2/5 twice, 3/5 four times). Soreness came back
**5/5 "none" on nine of ten check-ins** — she isn't accumulating enough stimulus to be sore,
which is a volume verdict. In-app stress reads 3–4/5, milder than the 7/10 at intake.

**Bloods (drawn 2026-07-06, age 29) — reframes the layoff.** Ferritin 82, TSH 1.3, T4 10.7,
B12 469, Hgb 12.8, HbA1c 4.5, FBS 92 — iron, thyroid, B12 and metabolic health all clean, which
rules out the usual fatigue explanations. Flagged: **25(OH) vitamin D 23 ng/ml (insufficient)** —
the plausible driver of the sleep-quality/energy picture, referred to her physician;
**CRP 8.8 (<6)** with **Lymph% 45.3** — mild inflammation, lymphocyte-predominant. That draw sits
**mid-layoff (Jun 29 → Jul 25)**, so the gap reads more like illness than disengagement — no
notes card treats it as a discipline failure. Cholesterol 209 / HDL 56 / TG 152: mild, supports
the C3 Metabolic Shift; deliberately *not* addressed with programmed conditioning this cycle
(adding cardio to a 45%-adherence hypertrophy block would cost the block). CA 125 6.2 — normal,
reassuring against the cyst. **No lab values appear in `data/elmira_askari.json` — it is public.**

**e1RM — low confidence, flagged to the athlete.** Her logging mixes formats (`20.10`, `00..10`,
`25`, `15.2`), conflating kg×reps with bare machine settings. Only two are usable:
**Barbell Hip Thrust ~40 kg**, **Barbell RDL ~27 kg** (both assuming bar included). A notes card
now teaches the format so C3's read isn't guesswork.

**Capacity: increase — but the ceiling is exposure, not recovery.** She needs more delt volume
and more completed sessions, not harder ones.

**Structure.** Amir held 4 days ("think of it as business" — it's what she's paying for), so the
adherence fix is structural instead: **every one of the four days now carries direct delt work.**
Whichever days she reaches, the priority muscle gets trained. Week order also front-loads value —
D1 (peak upper) and D2 (peak lower) are the two she historically completes.

**Roadmap.** No deviation. C2 "Growth Engine" already specifies shoulder + glute hypertrophy and
the three supported primaries (press, hip thrust, hack squat); all three stay.

**Stated exceptions (deliberate, not oversight).** Rotation ran at 25% carryover (3 of 12 C1
accessories), well under the 70% flag. But the library's delt-isolation pool is eight movements
(three without video) against 8 delt slots this cycle, so **Dumbbell Lateral Raise, Machine
Reverse Fly, Cable Face Pull and Cable Hip Abduction are kept under the isolation-slot exception**
— each with a visible dose progression rather than a worse substitute. **Cable Lateral Raise is
kept by Amir's direct instruction** (athlete preference). No supersets anywhere: several movements
are new to her and C1 gave no paired baselines.

**Considered and accepted:** 12 sets/week of loaded spinal flexion on a posture-goal client is a
real trade-off, taken because Amir specified crunch-family core and because the counterweight
(13 direct rear-delt sets + 11 back sets) heavily outweighs it.
