## Cycle 01 — Bedrock · 2026-08-07 · NEW

**The read**

*Recovery ceiling (gates everything).* Sleep 7–8h at 7/10 reads fine on paper, but acute
bereavement (father died ~1 month ago), a high-dose SSRI with ~5kg gain on it, and stress

constraint — recovery is. She arrives training 5 days/week at high load with no visible

priority muscles at the 10-set floor exactly, upper body pinned to the 4–8 retention band,
RPE hard cap 7, every week undulated. 5 loaded days exceeds that ceiling; the plateau at 5
days is the evidence.

*Priority targets (SFR-led, general fitness).* Glutes → Barbell Hip Thrust (her proven
strength and a competence anchor while grieving) + abduction + kickback. Quads → Machine Leg
Press leads (SFR over squat, and axial is deferred anyway) + extension + single-leg press.
Hamstrings/hinge → seated + lying curls plus the 45° Back Extension at hip-hinge ROM, which
is the actual project of this cycle. Upper → retention dose, pull/posture biased.

*Contraindications.* Past lower back, no current pain, no red flags. Trunk-supported hinge
only; her current Smith deadlift and squat are out this cycle — a fully pain-free C1 is the
permission slip for the C2 free hinge. Core is anti-movement brace work only, no loaded
lumbar flexion. Her lower-back flexibility goal is served the spine-safe way: unloaded
segmental mobility and hip/hamstring ROM, never loaded lumbar stretching on day one.
Grief + SSRI → RPE cap, B-version of every session, sessions reorderable. First-ever cardio
→ incline walking only, conversational, appended to lifts.

*Structure.* 3 full-body loaded days undulated peak/moderate/low, separated by 2 optional
restoration day-cards, so she keeps her 5-day gym ritual while only 3 days carry load.
Upper/lower was the obvious alternative and loses at 3 days: full-body gives 2–3 exposures
per priority muscle, cleaner B-version downgrades, and less cost when a day is missed.

*Roadmap check.* Matches C1 of the locked 5-cycle roadmap exactly. C1 is deliberately
maintenance/reset and is named as such to the athlete — success criteria exclude the scale.
