## Cycle 01 — Bedrock · 2026-08-07 · NEW

**The read**

*Recovery ceiling (gates everything).* Sleep 7–8h at 7/10 reads fine on paper, but acute
bereavement (father died ~1 month ago), a high-dose SSRI with ~5kg gain on it, and stress
8/10 put her real capacity well below that. Life load is light, so time is not the
constraint — recovery is. She arrives training 5 days/week at high load with no visible
change: a recovery problem wearing a plateau costume. Volume ceiling set BEFORE day count:
priority muscles at the 10-set floor exactly, upper body pinned to the 4–8 retention band,
RPE hard cap 7, every week undulated. 5 loaded days exceeds that ceiling; the plateau at 5
days is the evidence.

*Priority targets (SFR-led, general fitness).* Glutes → Barbell Hip Thrust (her proven
strength and a competence anchor while grieving) + abduction + kickback. Quads → Machine Leg
Press leads (SFR over squat, and axial is deferred anyway) + extension + single-leg press.
Hamstrings/hinge → seated + lying curls plus the 45° Back Extension at hip-hinge ROM, which
is the actual project of this cycle. Upper → retention dose, pull/posture biased.

*Contraindications.* Past lower back, no current pain, no red flags. Trunk-supported hinge
only; her current Smith deadlift and squat are out this cycle — a fully pain-free C1 is the
permission slip for the C2 free hinge. Core is anti-movement brace work only, no loaded
lumbar flexion. Her lower-back flexibility goal is served the spine-safe way: unloaded
segmental mobility and hip/hamstring ROM, never loaded lumbar stretching on day one.
Grief + SSRI → RPE cap, B-version of every session, sessions reorderable. First-ever cardio
→ incline walking only, conversational, appended to lifts.

*Structure.* 3 full-body loaded days undulated peak/moderate/low, separated by 2 optional
restoration day-cards, so she keeps her 5-day gym ritual while only 3 days carry load.
Upper/lower was the obvious alternative and loses at 3 days: full-body gives 2–3 exposures
per priority muscle, cleaner B-version downgrades, and less cost when a day is missed.

*Roadmap check.* Matches C1 of the locked 5-cycle roadmap exactly. C1 is deliberately
maintenance/reset and is named as such to the athlete — success criteria exclude the scale.

**Decisions**

```
PRIMARY LIFT SELECTIONS:
glutes/bridge    → Barbell Hip Thrust          (competence anchor, highest-SFR glute, spine-neutral)  — Day 1
quads/knee       → Machine Leg Press           (SFR > squat; axial deferred per back rule)            — Day 1
hinge/posterior  → 45° Back Extension          (trunk-supported groove; gates the C2 free hinge)      — Day 3
quads-unilateral → Single-Leg Machine Leg Press (supported low-day anchor, calibrates L/R)            — Day 5
```

Forks Amir settled at the checkpoint:
- **5 app day-cards, not 3.** Two restoration cards (walk + mobility, marked optional) rather
  than pushing the walks into a notes card. Keeps her 5-day identity inside the app and she
  logs them.
- **45° Back Extension stays in** with an adapt-later fallback rather than being designed
  around a station her gym might not have.
- **Day count is the coach's call.** Amir's framing: availability of 5 days is a ceiling, not
  an instruction to load her 5 times.
- **Cardio is a legitimate lever to introduce**, not something to hold back for a later cycle.
- **Period-week protocol confirmed** for this cycle's notes.

Audit history (both panels, per the standing multi-lens order):
- Roadmap panel (4 lenses + head-coach judge): `recovery-pragmatist` won 9.3/10; grafts taken
  from the fat-loss, physique-shape and spine-durability lenses.
- C1 design panel (clinical / house-rules / dose-time): 3 must-fix clinical items — the
  original 45° BE fallback was a seated *spinal-extension* machine (loaded lumbar
  articulation), there was no in-session back-pain protocol anywhere, and the daily brace/hinge
  ritual wasn't actually implemented so hinge patterning happened once a week. House-rules lint
  then found 6 more: **the week-4 back-off at "2 sets per exercise" broke all three volume
  floors (10→6)**, the SSRI clause making Day 5 optional broke them again (10→7), the
  no-station contingency was a dead instruction (it said "add a 4th set" to a lift already at
  4), and the RPE floor of 6 was breached in five places. All applied before assembly.

Week 1 is calibration at RPE 6 across the board; the RPE 7 cap unlocks from week 2. All
autoregulation reductions are floored at 6 (the app's selector floor). Week 4 deloads through
**load, not sets**, so the three volume floors survive the back-off week.

**C2 gate (the thing this cycle is actually buying):** 45° Back Extension at 3×12 loaded and
pain-free by week 3, plus a clean unloaded dowel-hinge screen in week 4. That unlocks the
light free hinge (DB/KB RDL as a skill) in Cycle 2. Any back symptom drops the pattern one
ladder step for the rest of the cycle, and the gate re-arms next cycle.
