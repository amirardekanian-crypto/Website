## Cycle 01 — Uncoil · 2026-08-08 · NEW

**The read**

*Recovery ceiling.* Sleep 7/10 at 7–8 hrs, stress 4/10, heavy life load, age 15 mid-maturation,
probable historic under-fuelling now being corrected. Court load is only ~6 hrs/wk across 5–6 days
plus an unquantified swim. **Volume is not the limiter** — energy availability, adolescence and an
uncounted third stressor are. Two training days at ~60 min gives ~26–32 working sets/week, so this
is a priority-and-maintenance design by arithmetic, not by timidity.

*Diagnosis (from the roadmap panel, and this cycle is its test).* Six hours a week did not break
him. His rotation budget is overdrawn and the overdraft is paid by the wrong joints: hip IR and
thoracic rotation are short, so the lumbar spine supplies rotation it was never built for and the
forearm muscles the racket head through contact because the chain never delivered it. The elbow was
the first invoice; the wrist is the second. The elbow was rehabbed, the sequencing never changed,
and the load walked one joint down. **Testable: restore hip IR and T-spine rotation and the wrist
should settle without ever being treated.**

*Contraindications → exercise level.* Active wrist → no wrist-loaded pressing, no front rack, straps
mandatory, no med-ball catches, no hand-supported landings, no carries, no rowing machine. Lumbar
disc + flexion intolerance → no loaded spinal flexion ever; leg press depth-capped before posterior
pelvic tilt; back extension capped at neutral at BOTH ends, because protecting flexion while loading
extension hard is the classic error in a young server. Cervical disc → no loaded neck flexion, no
neck extension under load, no barbell overhead press ever. ACL → reverse lunge only, bilateral before
single-leg, landings before jumps, nothing lateral until C5. Age 15 → no true 1RM, plyo dosed as
ground contacts.

*Structure.* Full-body ×2 + a real rehab day. Upper/lower would hit each pattern once a week, below
minimum effective frequency. Training days never back-to-back, rehab day as the buffer.

*Behavioural.* He trains through pain and under-reports. Every gate in this cycle resolves to a
measurement, a film or a third party — never his word.

**Decisions**

```
PRIMARY LIFT SELECTIONS
  Knee-dominant   → Machine Leg Press (depth-capped)      | SFR + zero axial load; fallback Hack Squat / Single-Leg Press, NEVER goblet or back squat
  Hinge           → Machine Hip Thrust (trunk-supported)  | brace before free hinge; fallback Glute Bridge, NEVER RDL
  Horizontal pull → Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row (straps) | SFR + zero lumbar demand
  Vertical pull   → Lat Pulldown (straps)                 | pull:press ≥3:1 per tennis/posture rule
  Unilateral knee → Dumbbell Reverse Lunge                | reverse only (ACL)
  Posterior chain → 45° Back Extension (neutral-capped)   | trunk-supported, second hinge exposure
```

*Transfer trade-off, stated:* the back squat is the higher-transfer choice for a tennis player and
was deliberately not used. C2 buys it with a pain-free cycle as the permission slip. Little lost
(leg press is high-SFR) and the attribution value is real — axial load enters as the only new
variable in C2.

*Forks Amir settled at the checkpoint:* gym equipment unconfirmed → assume leg press, hack squat as
fallback · include a minimal neutral-grip machine press rather than omitting pressing entirely ·
set the strength benchmark from week 1 observation rather than stated numbers ("trust the bar over
the résumé").

*Amir's earlier call, overridden by the audit panel:* he elected to program around the wrist with no
referral. All three auditors escalated it to must-fix, citing the principle added the same day — a
decision to program around pain needs a date and named escalation triggers or it stands forever by
silence. Shipped with a 2026-08-22 appointment date, six overriding triggers, and a scoped fallback
(the wrist variable freezes; the cycle does not repeat). **Amir was told and can pull it back out.**

*What the adversarial panel caught in the draft:* a rowing machine in the Day 3 warm-up (loaded
lumbar flexion at the catch + 5 min of grip on the active wrist + pronated pull on a tennis-elbow
history — the single largest unmanaged wrist exposure in the gym) · a Prone Y-Raise holding the
cervical spine in extension two lines below the note forbidding exactly that · Cat-Cow's deliberate
end-range lumbar flexion on the rehab day · a back-extension avoid cue guarding the top of the range
while leaving the loaded-flexion descent unguarded · and **cotton wool**: every set at RPE 7, no
strength benchmark, and zero knee-flexion hamstring work under an ACL knee about to start landing.
12 further findings were rejected as false positives.
