## Cycle 01 — Bedrock · 2026-08-01 · NEW

**The read** — Recovery-limited athlete (stress 9/10, sleep 5/10, heavy life load, 35y)
despite 3+ yrs training age: volume ceiling set at ~10-12 hard sets/wk for priorities,
RPE cap 7, before any session-count decision. Priorities: upper back/scap (posture goal
رفع قوز — noted, so correctives indicated), glutes/posterior chain via trunk-supported
hinge only (multi-year low-back history, resolved but standing), brace/anti-movement
core (Bedrock's graduation gates). Chronic-back rule applied in full: no loaded free
hinge anywhere incl. warm-ups (adversarial audit caught + removed a rowing-machine raise
that snuck a loaded hinge back in). 98kg → lat pulldown over chins, incline over floor
push-ups (floor ≈60kg press > his 40kg×10 bench, would've forced lumbar-extension
compensation under fatigue). Structure: 3× full-body undulated (high/moderate/low) —
each session self-contained at his life load; D3 = named minimum-dose day. Weight-loss
context (9kg in 50 days during a rough patch, maintained 20 days): all framing
build/health, scale not a coaching metric.

**Decisions** —
PRIMARY LIFT SELECTIONS:
  Glutes/hinge → Barbell Hip Thrust (trunk-supported, highest-SFR glute lift)
  Posterior chain → 45° Back Extension (hinge groove + erector endurance; BW week 1)
  Upper back → Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row (highest-SFR row, zero spinal cost)
  Quads → Machine Leg Press (AMIR'S CALL at checkpoint: Leg Press over Back Squat —
    safer for the low back; Back Squat re-enters C2 only if C1 fully pain-free — saved
    to COACHING-PRINCIPLES.md as a generalizable rule: axial loading defers a cycle too,
    not just the free hinge)
  Vertical press → Machine Shoulder Press (SFR hierarchy)
  Vertical pull → Lat Pulldown (98kg makes chins premature)

Adversarial audit panel (3 lenses) ran before this was shown to Amir. Must-fixes applied:
rowing-machine warm-up raise → Assault Bike (was smuggling a loaded hip hinge into a
cycle that bans it); floor Push-Up → Incline Push-Up (exceeded his demonstrated pressing
capacity — predictable hip-sag compensation loads the exact segment we're protecting);
Hip Thrust 4→3 sets (Day 1 was breaching the 45-min cap on honest arithmetic);
autoregulation rule given an explicit RPE floor of 6 (five prescriptions already sat at
6 — a blanket −1 would've sent them below the app's selectable range).
Should-fixes applied: Leg Press fallback Goblet Squat → Machine Hack Squat (fallback was
quietly re-admitting the free axial pattern the cycle deferred); Cable Seated Row avoid-cue
rewritten to guard the reach/loaded-flexion fault instead of lean-back (the fault that
actually threatens his back); added Dowel Hip Hinge to Day 3 prep (unloaded pattern
rehearsal — C2 loads a hinge he'd otherwise not have practiced in 4 weeks); published
honest pull:press arithmetic (11:5 ≈ 2.2:1 overall, 11:2 vs horizontal — the 2 horizontal
sets are deliberate closed-chain serratus work, which helps rather than fights the
posture goal).
