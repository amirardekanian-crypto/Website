## Cycle 01 — Bedrock · 2026-08-01 · NEW

**The read** — Recovery-limited athlete (stress 9/10, sleep 5/10, heavy life load, 35y)
despite 3+ yrs training age: volume ceiling set at ~10-12 hard sets/wk for priorities,
RPE cap 7, before any session-count decision. Priorities: upper back/scap (posture goal
رفع قوز — noted, so correctives indicated), glutes/posterior chain via trunk-supported
hinge only (multi-year low-back history, resolved but standing), brace/anti-movement
core (Bedrock's graduation gates). Chronic-back rule applied in full: no loaded free
hinge anywhere incl. warm-ups (adversarial audit caught + removed a rowing-machine raise
that snuck a loaded hinge back in). 98kg → lat pulldown over chins, incline over floor
push-ups (floor ≈60kg press > his 40kg×10 bench, would've forced lumbar-extension
compensation under fatigue). Structure: 3× full-body undulated (high/moderate/low) —
each session self-contained at his life load; D3 = named minimum-dose day. Weight-loss
context (9kg in 50 days during a rough patch, maintained 20 days): all framing
build/health, scale not a coaching metric.

**Decisions** —
PRIMARY LIFT SELECTIONS:
  Glutes/hinge → Barbell Hip Thrust (trunk-supported, highest-SFR glute lift)
  Posterior chain → 45° Back Extension (hinge groove + erector endurance; BW week 1)
  Upper back → Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row (highest-SFR row, zero spinal cost)
  Quads → Machine Leg Press (AMIR'S CALL at checkpoint: Leg Press over Back Squat —
    safer for the low back; Back Squat re-enters C2 only if C1 fully pain-free — saved
    to COACHING-PRINCIPLES.md as a generalizable rule: axial loading defers a cycle too,
    not just the free hinge)
  Vertical press → Machine Shoulder Press (SFR hierarchy)
  Vertical pull → Lat Pulldown (98kg makes chins premature)

Adversarial audit panel (3 lenses) ran before this was shown to Amir. Must-fixes applied:
rowing-machine warm-up raise → Assault Bike (was smuggling a loaded hip hinge into a
cycle that bans it); floor Push-Up → Incline Push-Up (exceeded his demonstrated pressing
capacity — predictable hip-sag compensation loads the exact segment we're protecting);
Hip Thrust 4→3 sets (Day 1 was breaching the 45-min cap on honest arithmetic);
autoregulation rule given an explicit RPE floor of 6 (five prescriptions already sat at
6 — a blanket −1 would've sent them below the app's selectable range).
Should-fixes applied: Leg Press fallback Goblet Squat → Machine Hack Squat (fallback was
quietly re-admitting the free axial pattern the cycle deferred); Cable Seated Row avoid-cue
rewritten to guard the reach/loaded-flexion fault instead of lean-back (the fault that
actually threatens his back); added Dowel Hip Hinge to Day 3 prep (unloaded pattern
rehearsal — C2 loads a hinge he'd otherwise not have practiced in 4 weeks); published
honest pull:press arithmetic (11:5 ≈ 2.2:1 overall, 11:2 vs horizontal — the 2 horizontal
sets are deliberate closed-chain serratus work, which helps rather than fights the
posture goal).

   station's busy or anything feels off: **Reverse Hyperextension** (fully trunk-supported,
   the conservative option).

   designed, re-check whether "DB RDL grooved" is still a genuine graduation or now a
   repeat of Cable Pull Through — pick the actual next step from how C1 actually went,
   not from the roadmap's original wording.
4. **Consider saving as a principle:** squat-tolerance ≠ hinge-tolerance for a chronic-back
   client — a pain-free squat is good news but doesn't clear a loaded free hinge on its
   own, because the two patterns load the lumbar spine differently. Pending Amir's OK.

## Cycle 01 — Bedrock (HOME REWRITE) · 2026-08-19 · RETURNING (rewrite in place)

**Amir's decisions at the STEP 1 checkpoint:** rewrite C1 in place (`currentCycleIndex` stays 0,
cycle dates unchanged 2026-08-01→08-28, no `programHistory` advance) · design defensively rather

**The read** — Athlete quit the gym entirely and moved home; every one of C1's 15 movements became
impossible at once. Two corrections to the obvious reading of his data drove the design:

1. **The adherence story is not a fade.** Aug 2/5/7/9 = 4 sessions in 8 days = 3.5/wk, ABOVE his
   3/wk prescription. Then a hard stop and 8 days of silence. His failure mode is BINARY — an
   event, not a decline — which kills the case for hedging the architecture against partial weeks.
   The cause is still unknown and that is the single most important open item (see handoff).
2. **The RPE drop is CONFOUNDED and must not be banked on.** Leg press 30/50/60kg @ 7,8,9 in a
   session logged at 15 min → identical loads @ 7,7,7 in a 43-min session. 13 working sets cannot
   happen in 15 minutes (prescribed rests alone total 12–19 min); the app timer is a manual
   stopwatch started late. Compressed rest inflates RPE, full rest deflates it. The home block
   therefore does NOT open heavy on the assumption he adapted.

Recovery picture revised UP: intake said sleep 5/10, but his own Proof habit log shows **7 / 7.5 /
8 / 7.5 hours**. Steps 500–6,000/day (sedentary) is the most fixable number in his file and is now
a daily prescription. Back silent across all 4 sessions incl. a self-selected pain-free 60kg squat;
the safety question C1 existed to answer got answered. **The brace did NOT happen** — Cable Pallof
Press was programmed all cycle and performed zero times (crunch machine substituted twice). It
failed on VENUE, not capacity, and a band Pallof at home has no excuse in it.

**He breaches written instructions.** RPE 10 on shoulder press and 8s on rows against a cap of 7;
left the leg press static despite a notes card telling him to add load; added his own 60kg squat
against a card titled "You'll want to go heavier. Don't." Consequence for design: countermeasures
must be **countable** (spacing rules, session ceilings, logged integers, photo gates), not
exhortations. Also worth noting: there is no barbell at home, so the environment now enforces what
the writing could not.

**Loadability finding that reframed the block** — his 60kg leg press was delivering only ~27–30kg
per leg. RFESS at bodyweight is ~60–70kg per leg, comparable to the 60kg back squat he already did
pain-free. Home is a STEP UP for knee-dominant, horizontal pull, anti-rotation core and calves; a
real DOWNGRADE only for vertical pull, vertical press and loaded carry; and the 45° Back Extension
has no home equivalent at all (Bird Dog is the least-bad cover).
