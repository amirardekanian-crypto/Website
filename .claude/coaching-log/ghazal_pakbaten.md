## Cycle 02 — Strength Engine · 2026-08-21 · RETURNING
*(first logged cycle — Cycle 1 predates this log)*

**The read**

*Adaptation.* Cycle 1 was a 5-day program. She logged **3 sessions, all in week 1** (Apr 25 D1
RPE 5, Apr 27 D3 RPE 7, Apr 30 D4 RPE 6), then nothing for ~16 weeks. New intake landed
2026-08-19. So the adaptation data is one week deep and has to be read as a capacity snapshot,
not a trend. Nothing overwhelmed her — session RPEs 5/6/7 against RPE 6–7 targets. The one
signal that matters is the **spread between lifts**: Lat Pulldown 110lb×8 @ RPE 6 (e1RM ~63kg)
against Half-Kneeling DB Press dropped 20→15lb @ RPE 7 (e1RM ~9kg/arm) and Barbell Chest Press
35lb×10 @ RPE 7 (~21kg). Fixed-path pulling is strong, free-weight pressing is well behind.
At 90kg bodyweight her back squat top set (80lb×10, e1RM ~48kg) is ~0.4× bodyweight for 10 —
beginner-intermediate despite "3+ years" on the form. *Trust the bar over the résumé.*

*Athlete request.* She flagged **Lateral Step-Down** twice in the same report ("too hard for
me, would be nice to have an alternative"). Removed and ledger-banned.

*Recovery & lifestyle.* This is the governing constraint. Sleep quality 8 but **5–6 hours**;
stress **9/10**; nutrition unstructured; 90kg; padel 1–2 days. Classic poor-recovery profile
where per-day load distribution beats total volume. It is also the most plausible reason a
5-day program lasted one week. **Consequence: 4 mandatory days + 1 genuinely optional day**
(Amir's call, and the data backs it), and the lower-body volume floor is met entirely by the
4 mandatory days so the optional day can be skipped without cost.

*Injury.* Form says Past Injuries = Knee, Current Injury = Yes with the detail box empty.
Amir confirmed at the checkpoint: **nothing active, old knee only.** Standing knee rails kept
(reverse over forward lunge, stick landings, pain-guided depth, pre-session knee check).
Cycle 1's notes were built around a **lumbar disc history** that her own new intake does not
list; treated as not currently restricting, so the free hinge enters this cycle — but led with
DB rather than barbell and partnered with a trunk-supported 45° Back Extension in the same
session. Flagged to Amir in the handoff.

*Capacity.* Hold session count at 4, increase intensity (RPE 7→8 on primaries), decrease reps
(10 → 6–8), increase unilateral share. Week 1 is explicitly a recalibration week after 16
weeks off — carried in the notes, not baked into a lower card RPE.

*Roadmap.* Cycle 2 = Strength Engine, weeks 6–10, "moderate loads RPE 7–8, unilateral emphasis,
tempo-controlled plyometrics." Fits as written. No deviation.
