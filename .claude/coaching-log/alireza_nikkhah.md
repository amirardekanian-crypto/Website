## Cycle 01 — Hard Stop · 2026-08-17 · NEW

**The read** — 38yo male, 88 kg, tennis. Intake form claimed **Professional**; Amir confirmed
he is not, which is the single most consequential fact in the file. Under "trust the bar over
the résumé" that inflation contaminates *every* self-reported field on the same form, so the
"3+ years gym experience" and especially the "Plyometrics" checkbox are all treated as
unverified. Baselines were left blank entirely.

⚠️ The same logic was initially applied selectively — the injury history ("no injuries, no past
injuries") was promoted to fact and printed as "clean sheet" while everything else from that
form was quarantined. The clinical auditor caught it. A 38-year-old playing 10–15 h/week with a
genuinely blank injury sheet is uncommon, and athletes routinely omit a niggle they play
through. Downgraded to "no injuries **reported**, unverified", with one symptom question in the
week-1 notes card (history, not examination — ROM assessment stays out of scope).

**Recovery ceiling, set before session count.** Stress 2/10, light life-load, nutrition on
track, 7–8 h sleep — good on paper. Two debits: sleep **quality 5/10**, and age 38, which
specifically slows recovery from the high-eccentric and high-CNS work this cycle trades in.
But the real gate is that **10–15 h of court is already a large load before he touches a
barbell**. Court saturates four qualities — trunk rotation, overhead/serving, high-velocity
SSC, and conditioning — so the gym's only job is what the court cannot supply: high force,
applied slowly, under control. Ceiling set at ~38 cost-weighted units/week with the binding
constraint on the **heavy end** (≤8 RPE 8+ compound sets/week, ≤4 per session), not the total.
Three days is both his limit and the correct answer; the arithmetic and his availability agree.

**Equipment forced the design in a helpful direction.** CrossFit gym — barbells, DBs, KBs,
pull-up bar, flat bench, rings/TRX, GHD, landmine, jump box, bands, med balls, ergs. **No
machines, no cables, no olympic lifts** (Amir's call: no technique, so power comes from throws,
jumps and bounds). Losing the machines pushed selection exactly where the principles already
say a sport athlete belongs — transfer over SFR, free-standing over supported. Priced
explicitly: paid for by deleting horizontal press, holding overhead out, capping heavy sets and
keeping total sets low.

**Structure.** Full-body ×3, each day owning a distinct lower-body pattern (bilateral knee /
hinge / unilateral). Beats Lower/Upper/Lower because for a concurrent athlete distribution is
the primary lever, and distinct daily patterns guarantee no two high-load days hit the same
pattern back to back. Undulated 13.5 / 13.0 / 11.5 units, with the sharper signal being the
heavy-set split 4 / 3 / 0.

**Decisions**
```
PRIMARY LIFT SELECTIONS
  Bilateral knee    → Barbell Back Squat (tempo eccentric)   [fallback: Goblet Squat]
  Hinge / posterior → Barbell Romanian Deadlift (tempo)      [fallback: Dumbbell RDL]
  Unilateral        → Dumbbell Split Squat (tempo)           [fallback: Split Squat BW]
  Horizontal pull   → Single-Arm Dumbbell Row / TRX Row
  Vertical pattern  → Scapular Pull-Up (on-ramp only)

Forks Amir settled at the checkpoint:
  · Back Squat over Front Squat as the bilateral primary. My recommendation was Front Squat
    (self-limiting, teaches the upright deceleration posture, lower axial load on unverified
    technique); Amir chose Back Squat. Consequence: the film gate and the on-card ramp/rack-
    safety/depth instructions carry more weight than they otherwise would.
  · Pulling starts on rings/TRX rather than assuming pull-up capacity at 88 kg. This removed
    vertical pulling entirely, so Scapular Pull-Up was added as the on-ramp.
```

**Adversarial panel — 56 findings, 18 must-fix, all applied.** Three were genuinely serious and
invisible in the spec's own accounting:
1. **Day 3 was labelled LOW while stacking three Achilles loaders** — jump rope, pogos, and
   paused end-range single-leg calf raises, ~480 impact contacts plus 144 s of loaded
   end-range Achilles eccentric, in the one athlete population where that tendon is the
   signature overuse injury. Fixed at the root: jump rope out (never confirmed he owns one),
   landings redistributed one per day, **direct calf work cut entirely** — court saturates the
   calf and Pogo Jump supplies the elastic stimulus.
2. **The film gate governed progression, not entry.** Session 1 was 4×5 @ RPE 8 with a 3s
   eccentric under a bar nobody had seen him hold, with no ramp protocol and no rack-safety
   instruction anywhere in the file. Ramp, safeties and a depth rule are now permanent on-card
   instructions; the bottom pause was dropped (3-0-1-0, not 3-1-1-0) as risk without stimulus.
3. **Minimum effective dose was backwards on risk** — it preserved the RPE 8 barbell squat on
   exactly the days he'd declared himself under-recovered and deleted the harmless accessories.
   Rewritten to shed load: primary runs at its fallback or 2 sets, and a low-readiness day
   never carries a barbell at RPE 8.

Also caught: **Chest-Supported DB Row needs an incline bench** and he has flat only (swapped to
Single-Arm DB Row, hand and knee supported — a bent-over row would have re-loaded the lumbar on
squat day, which is what the slot existed to avoid); the heavy-set count was **under-counted by
nearly half** (TRX Row at RPE 8 is a compound: 7/week, not 4); and Day 1's real time was 50.3
min against a 45–50 wall.

**Exercise Ledger Updates** — new athlete, ledger created. All 15 programmed exercises →
`Active`. Five recorded as `Available` with deferral reasons so a future cycle knows they were
considered and consciously held back rather than never thought of: Nordic Hamstring Curl, Depth
Jump, Single-Leg Bound, Medicine Ball Rotational Throw (all deferred at design), and Standing
Dumbbell Single Leg Calf Raise (cut post-audit).

**Progression levers**
- Barbell Back Squat: film-gated. Cleared film + all 4×5 at/under RPE 8 → +2.5–5 kg. The arc's
  primary progression driver into Force Bank.
- Barbell RDL: film-gated. Lever is the eccentric, not the load. Clean 3s eccentric on all 4
  sets → +2.5–5 kg. Any rounding = hold the load.
- DB Split Squat: 3×8 each leg at RPE 7 with no front-knee drift → +2 kg per DB.
- TRX Row: walk the feet forward one step when 3×10 lands at/under RPE 8.
- Scapular Pull-Up: 2×6 clean → build toward 2×10, then assisted pull-up in C2.
- Deload call: readiness under 3 for two consecutive sessions → cut both Day 1 carries and drop
  to 2 sets on every primary that week.

**e1RM** — none. New athlete, no logged sets. Week 1 of this cycle establishes the first
baseline on all three primaries; every future cycle reads its e1RM trend from here.
