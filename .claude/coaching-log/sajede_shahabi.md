## Cycle 01 — Plumb Line · 2026-09-07 · NEW

**The read**

Recovery is split and the split is the whole design. Sleep 7-8h at 8/10, age 29, no injury,
nutrition on track — that body recovers from far more than she is about to do. Stress 10/10
with a heavy life load caps psychological capacity and makes attendance fragile, but it does
not cap tissue. So the volume ceiling came off a stopwatch, not off her physiology:
45 min − 12 min mandatory prep = 33 min working, ÷ ~2.5 min per set for a true beginner in a
busy commercial gym = 13 sets a session, 26 a week for the whole body. That number forces
every trade in this cycle. At ~5-6 sets/muscle/week a true beginner captures most of the
available adaptation, so this dose is genuinely productive for her — it is not a compromise
and it must not be sold as one.

Priority targets are SFR-led. She led her intake with posture, not the checkboxes, so
horizontal pull runs twice a week and vertical pull once — rounded shoulders and thoracic
kyphosis are a mid-back and rear-delt problem, and a lat-dominant programme can feed the
internally-rotated position she is complaining about. Zero working sets go to corrective
drills; the postural dose rides in the prep block, the home days, and the execution standard
on ordinary lifts.

Contraindications are unusual here in that there are none of the normal kind. No pain, no
injury, no restriction — Amir confirmed the posture concern is cosmetic. The real risks are
dropout, a wrecked first week from eccentric damage, and being trapped alone under a machine
she has never used. All three are designed against explicitly.

Structure: full body both days. Upper/lower loses badly at 2 sessions — a split gives each
muscle one exposure, so one missed session deletes half her body for a week, and a missed
session is a certainty at some point on a 10/10 stress load. Full body halves a bad week
instead of zeroing it. Straight sets throughout per the first-cycle superset ban.

**Decisions**

```
PRIMARY LIFT SELECTIONS
  Knee-dominant    → Machine Leg Press (D1) · Machine Hack Squat (D2)
  Hip / glute      → Machine Hip Thrust (D1) · 45° Back Extension (D2)
  Hamstring        → Machine Seated Leg Curl (D1)
  Horizontal pull  → Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row (D1) · Cable Seated Row (D2)  [2x/wk]
  Vertical pull    → Lat Pulldown (D2)  [1x/wk, deliberately sub-dominant]
  Core             → Dead Bug (D1) · Kneeling Side Plank (D2)
```

Forks Amir settled at the checkpoints:
- 2 gym days is the fixed contract; home mobility + a step habit carry flexibility and daily
  movement. Not negotiated upward.
- Posture is cosmetic, no pain, no restrictions. Correctives permitted on the noted indication.
- Period-week protocol confirmed for this cycle.
- Full gym confirmed — specialty machines assumed available; fallbacks kept for busy stations.
- Rear delts held to the roadmap (direct dose arrives C2) rather than spending C1 sets. Prep
  carries daily scapular/rear-delt exposure at zero working-set cost.
- Off-library names kept and Amir films them, per his standing rule that a missing video never
  shapes the prescription.
- Nutrition is habits-only (protein + steps). No intake prescription. Fat loss is therefore
  honest but slower, and that is stated to her rather than implied away.
- Week-1 baseline is tape only (waist, hips, thigh, arm) — no photos.

Coach's own call, not asked: moved one set from hip thrust to seated leg curl (2/3 rather than
3/2). Hamstrings at 3.5 was too thin even inside a declared deviation; glutes still clear 7.0
from four separate sources.

**Audit panel — 6 must-fix applied, 3 findings declined**

1. Both squat fallbacks were Smith Machine Squat — a free-standing barbell squat in a
   machines-only cycle, fired precisely on the busy day when a fallback is used. The two
   machines now fall back to each other, so she never meets an untaught lift under pressure.
2. Barbell Hip Thrust fallback on the anti-arch lift. → Dumbbell Glute Bridge (floor-based).
3. Neither leg-machine note mentioned safety stops or release handles. She trains alone.
   Added as the first line of both.
4. Week 1's only protection was RPE, which does not control eccentric damage. Week 1 now runs
   2 sets and a 2s lowering; Machine Seated Leg Curl dropped to RPE 7 permanently.
5. Load-identity labels were backwards (D2 = 13.5 vs D1 = 12.0). Relabelled.
6. Day 2 prep ran 2 min longer than Day 1's, on the day that breached the cap. Trimmed.

Declined, with reasons: swapping the 45° Back Extension for a seated machine version (deletes
hamstring volume from the audit column — took the 3→2 dose cut instead); a Beighton
hypermobility screen (ROM assessment, out of scope per "coaching is not diagnosis"); a static
hip-flexor stretch in prep (the roadmap's refusal list bans static stretching pre-lift — used a
dynamic half-kneeling rock instead, which also teaches the posterior tilt the hip thrust card
demands).

**Progression levers**

- Machine Leg Press / Machine Hack Squat — lever: load at a fixed 3-0-1-0 tempo. Add-trigger:
  10 clean reps at or under RPE 7 with the low back flat on the pad for all 3 sets, **verified on
  film**. Increment: smallest plate on the stack. Deload: hold and repeat, don't drop.
- Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row / Cable Seated Row — lever: load, then reps to 12. Add-trigger:
  12 reps at RPE 8 with no shrug and no forward roll on the return.
- Lat Pulldown — lever: load. Add-trigger: 12 reps at RPE 8 with no lean-back and no lift off
  the seat. Watch this one: lifting off the seat is the tell that she has outrun the thigh pad,
  not that she is strong.
- Machine Seated Leg Curl / Machine Hip Thrust / 45° Back Extension — lever: load. Add-trigger:
  all sets at target reps, RPE at or under card.
- Kneeling Side Plank — lever: time. Add-trigger: 30s clean each side, then the full-lever Side
  Plank replaces it.

All progression is coach-driven from her logs. No self-progression rule reaches the athlete.

**Strength benchmark (weeks 4-5)** — the cycle defers horizontal press, overhead press and all
free/axial loading, so the effort has to be real inside what she owns: a clean Machine Leg Press
at 10 reps, RPE 8, low back flat on the pad for all three sets; and a clean Lat Pulldown at 12
reps, RPE 8, no lean-back and no lift off the seat. Clearing both opens Cycle 2's progression.

**e1RM** — none. No logged sets exist; this is her first cycle. First e1RM read comes at the

## Cycle 01 — Plumb Line · REVISED 2026-08-31 · 2 days → 3 days

> Appended, not edited. The section above is the original 2-day reasoning and stays as written.
> This records what the constraint change did to it, before the cycle ever started.

**What changed and why**

The athlete told Amir she can train **3 days a week, not 2**, before week 1 began. That moves
the only number the whole design was derived from: 45 min − ~11 min prep ÷ ~2.5 min/set was
13 sets × 2 days = **26 sets/week**; at three days it is **41**. Amir's three calls: 3 × full
body with a trimmed third day · third day **mandatory** from week 1 · roadmap revised directly
rather than re-running the roadmap panel.

**The declared weeks 1-10 lower-body floor deviation is GONE.** It was justified on arithmetic
— 26 sets could not cover the ≥10 floor for quads/hams/glutes *and* the upper back she led her
intake with. At 41 it can. All three now sit at exactly **10.0** from week 2. There is no
margin: any week Day 3 is missed, quads and hams fall back to 6.0 and the deviation returns.
That is why Day 3 is mandatory and why the notes card refuses to nominate it as droppable.

Week 1 is a **one-week stated deviation** (2 sets of everything → quads 6.0, hams 6.0,
glutes 8.0). Deliberate calibration, not a design gap.

**What survived untouched:** the postural thesis. Posture is a hypertrophy and
strength-through-range outcome, zero working sets to corrective drills, horizontal pull over
vertical, machines-only. None of that was a function of day count.

**Roadmap revision (Amir's call, direct rather than re-panelled)**
- C1: 2 → 3 days; lower-body floor now clears here rather than at week 11.
- C2: dropped "first real upper-back dose" — direct rear-delt work moved into C1.
- C3: job changed from "pairing buys the lower-body floor" (floor now clears in C1) to
  **pairing for density only**. Tagline → "More work, same clock".
- C4: **absorbed the free-weight transition** from C3. The audit was right that C3 would
  otherwise stack her first superset and her first free weight into one block, immediately
  after a C2 teaser promising "nothing new to learn".
- C5: unchanged. Still 5 × 5.

**Day 3 (new) — the low day.** Machine Reverse Fly 3 · Machine Leg Extension 4 · Machine Lying
Leg Curl 4 · Seated Machine Hip Abduction 3. 14 sets, cost-weighted 7.0 against 12.5 / 13.5,
and the **shortest** session of the week (~40 min vs ~41.5 / ~41). Reverse Fly sits **first**
deliberately: it is her headline complaint, it is the only direct rear-delt work in the cycle,
and last position is where things get skipped.

**Second adversarial panel — 8 must-fix applied**
1. **Cable Glute Kickback removed** (all three lenses). Only unsupported/unilateral working lift
   in a machines-only cycle; hip extension past ~15° comes from lumbar extension, the exact
   pattern four other cards coach her out of; its own cue said "back **and up**"; its fallback
   was a different muscle. → Seated Machine Hip Abduction (seated, bilateral, glute medius —
   a different glute function from the two glute-max lifts already in the week).
2. **Leg extension fallback reopened the safety-stop hole** the first audit closed, and
   prescribed "feet low and close" — the riskiest leg-press foot position. Rewritten.
3. **"Press until your legs are straight"** contradicted Day 1's "knees slightly soft at the
   top" for the same joint. → "stop just short of locking your knees". Leg extension also
   dropped to RPE 7 (first open-chain knee loading).
4. **Lying leg curl note said "stays at RPE 7 all cycle"** — an override against three
   autoregulation cards, using a mechanism she has never been taught. Deleted.
5. **"Every leg muscle on a full week of work from week one"** was false (week 1 is 2 sets).
   → "from week two".
6. **"If a week falls apart, Day 3 is the one to lose"** contradicted the mandatory decision
   AND the entire floor claim rests on Day 3. → short version, never dropped.
7. **The 20-minute promise computed to ~28 min.** → "2 sets of each and one warm-up round".
8. **Week 1 → week 2 was a single jump** from 2 sets/RPE 6/2s to 4 sets/RPE 8/3s across 41
   sets. → middle rung: week 2 runs full sets but keeps the 2s lowering and caps RPE 7.

Also applied: hard "never two gym days back to back" (all three days are leg days now, and the
old rule only guarded the D1/D2 pair); Day 3 prep reworked to actually prime the day (bodyweight
Squat in, Cat-Cow out — 8 of 14 sets are knee work and nothing prepped the knee); a new
**"Being Sore Is Normal"** card (sedentary athlete, 41 sets, 3s eccentrics, alone, abroad, and
nothing in the file answered the question she is most likely to ask in week 2); leg-curl
cross-fallbacks capped at 3 sets so a busy machine cannot produce 8 sets of one movement; prep
circuits levelled to ~11 min and compound rest 120 → 105s to buy back the clock.

**Declined:** running the week D1 → D3 → D2 (the dose lens wanted the light day in the middle;
the clinical lens showed that puts 4 sets of lying curl 48h before the back extension and 4 sets
of leg extension 48h before the hack squat — the gap is the lever, not the order).
