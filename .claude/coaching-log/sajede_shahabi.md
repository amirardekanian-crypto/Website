## Cycle 01 — Plumb Line · 2026-09-07 · NEW

**The read**

Recovery is split and the split is the whole design. Sleep 7-8h at 8/10, age 29, no injury,
nutrition on track — that body recovers from far more than she is about to do. Stress 10/10
with a heavy life load caps psychological capacity and makes attendance fragile, but it does
not cap tissue. So the volume ceiling came off a stopwatch, not off her physiology:
45 min − 12 min mandatory prep = 33 min working, ÷ ~2.5 min per set for a true beginner in a
busy commercial gym = 13 sets a session, 26 a week for the whole body. That number forces
every trade in this cycle. At ~5-6 sets/muscle/week a true beginner captures most of the
available adaptation, so this dose is genuinely productive for her — it is not a compromise
and it must not be sold as one.

Priority targets are SFR-led. She led her intake with posture, not the checkboxes, so
horizontal pull runs twice a week and vertical pull once — rounded shoulders and thoracic
kyphosis are a mid-back and rear-delt problem, and a lat-dominant programme can feed the
internally-rotated position she is complaining about. Zero working sets go to corrective
drills; the postural dose rides in the prep block, the home days, and the execution standard
on ordinary lifts.

Contraindications are unusual here in that there are none of the normal kind. No pain, no
injury, no restriction — Amir confirmed the posture concern is cosmetic. The real risks are
dropout, a wrecked first week from eccentric damage, and being trapped alone under a machine
she has never used. All three are designed against explicitly.

Structure: full body both days. Upper/lower loses badly at 2 sessions — a split gives each
muscle one exposure, so one missed session deletes half her body for a week, and a missed
session is a certainty at some point on a 10/10 stress load. Full body halves a bad week
instead of zeroing it. Straight sets throughout per the first-cycle superset ban.

**Progression levers**

- Machine Leg Press / Machine Hack Squat — lever: load at a fixed 3-0-1-0 tempo. Add-trigger:
  10 clean reps at or under RPE 7 with the low back flat on the pad for all 3 sets, **verified on
  film**. Increment: smallest plate on the stack. Deload: hold and repeat, don't drop.
- Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row / Cable Seated Row — lever: load, then reps to 12. Add-trigger:
  12 reps at RPE 8 with no shrug and no forward roll on the return.
- Lat Pulldown — lever: load. Add-trigger: 12 reps at RPE 8 with no lean-back and no lift off
  the seat. Watch this one: lifting off the seat is the tell that she has outrun the thigh pad,
  not that she is strong.
- Machine Seated Leg Curl / Machine Hip Thrust / 45° Back Extension — lever: load. Add-trigger:
  all sets at target reps, RPE at or under card.
- Kneeling Side Plank — lever: time. Add-trigger: 30s clean each side, then the full-lever Side
  Plank replaces it.

All progression is coach-driven from her logs. No self-progression rule reaches the athlete.

**Strength benchmark (weeks 4-5)** — the cycle defers horizontal press, overhead press and all
free/axial loading, so the effort has to be real inside what she owns: a clean Machine Leg Press
at 10 reps, RPE 8, low back flat on the pad for all three sets; and a clean Lat Pulldown at 12
reps, RPE 8, no lean-back and no lift off the seat. Clearing both opens Cycle 2's progression.
