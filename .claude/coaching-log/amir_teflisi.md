## Cycle 01 — Bare Metal · 2026-08-17 · NEW

**⚠️ Roadmap corrected 2026-08-17.** This first shipped as 6 cycles of 4/5/5/4/6/5 (29 weeks)
because the multi-lens panel argued the arc from the science and nobody checked it against the
house rule. Amir: *"the rule is 5 cycles of 5 weeks and you need to remember that."* Now
**5 × 5 = 25 weeks**: Bare Metal · Rebar · Keystone · Ballast · Foundry, ending 2027-02-07.
Foundry absorbs the old Load Bearing's job (re-test, restrictions re-stamped, the landing).
C1 gained a week, which is a clinical gain — five weeks of isometric plus eccentric on the
elbow before C2's heavy slow resistance, rather than four. The week-6 clinician gate now falls
exactly on C2's opening day, 2026-09-21. Rule written into COACHING-PRINCIPLES.md → Process
and into program-roadmap/SKILL.md so it is not re-derived next time.

**The read**
Recovery is the gate and sleep is the whole of it: 5–6 h against a self-rating of 9/10, so he
doesn't perceive the deficit and it can't be fixed by conversation — only tracked as a number
tied to outcomes he cares about. Stress 4/10 and a moderate life load are genuinely fine,
which is why 3 hard days is a real ceiling rather than pessimism. Volume ceiling set at ~10–12
hard sets/priority muscle/week — bottom of the evidence range, because a short sleeper in a
deficit doesn't have the budget for sets 13–20 and most of the benefit sits at 10.

Training age is a paper number: 3 years of machine circuits at low intent means high
responsiveness and near-zero free-weight skill, so frequency beats per-session volume and
every pattern gets 2–3 exposures. Structure is 3 × full-body, not a split — brand-new motor
skills need exposures, and a missed session costs a third of everything rather than all of one
thing, which matters in month one. PPL was the alternative and loses on both counts.

Three injury sites, all governed. Back = no axial load (supported knee-dominant primary,
trunk-supported hinge). Shoulder = nothing above shoulder height anywhere, which cost the
entire vertical-pull pattern this cycle. Elbow = occupationally driven (desk/phone, confirmed
by Amir), so the highest-yield intervention is his workstation, not his programme — gym
exposure is ~3 h/wk against ~50 h of the real provocation.

**Decisions**
PRIMARY LIFT SELECTIONS — knee-dominant: Machine Leg Press (SFR + chronic-back rule, matches
the Faraz C1 ruling) · hip-dominant: 45° Back Extension + Machine Hip Thrust (trunk-supported,
builds the brace that becomes C3's permission slip) · horizontal press: Machine Chest Press ·
horizontal pull: Chest-Supported DB Row · vertical pull: NONE — dropped entirely, no vertical
pull stays below shoulder height · vertical press: DEFERRED to C3 · elbow: daily home isometric
+ eccentric loading from wk 2 · shoulder: Band Pull-Apart, scap-first, below shoulder height.

Forks Amir settled at the checkpoint: arms = one neutral low-grip item · easy days =
prescribed sessions AND a Proof habit · NO test day, everything runs off RPE (his standing
"light testing only" principle — the design panel's proposed filmed AMRAP was overruled) ·
elbow loading pulled forward into wk 2 rather than waiting for C2, because progressive loading
is the best-evidence intervention for lateral epicondylalgia and isometric-only would spend
four weeks not building capacity · nutrition stays habit-level via Proof, no meal plan.

The hammer curl he originally approved was corrected out on clinical grounds after the audit —
brachioradialis originates on the lateral supracondylar ridge, continuous with the common
extensor origin, so it is an aggravator rather than the sparing pick it was sold as. Cable Curl
with straps preserves his intent (one neutral, low-grip arm item) without the provocation. The
"canary" framing was dropped too: deliberately provoking the elbow while running a daily
analgesic isometric makes the isometric unreadable.

**Audit corrections (adversarial panel, 3 lenses, 14 clinical must-fixes)**
The first draft declared "below shoulder height all cycle" and then breached it four times —
Lat Pulldown (starts in full overhead elevation), the Y of the Y-T-W (loaded end-range
overhead), a row cued "to the hips" (humerus into extension behind the torso), and a lateral
raise capped AT shoulder height, which is the centre of the painful arc rather than below it.
A prep-circuit leak put 16 end-range lumbar flexion cycles a day (Cat-Cow ×2 rounds × 8) into a
flexion-sensitive back the leg-press note explicitly protects. The leg-press fallback was
Machine Hack Squat, whose shoulder pads re-admitted the exact axial loading the cycle exists to
defer. Day 5 ran the back extension BEFORE the heaviest leg press, fatiguing the erectors that
enforce the position rule. The autoregulation MED protected the back extension (the riskiest
lift) and dropped the rehab. Cost-weighted load was flat at 10.8% spread across three days —
the specific failure mode the house rule names as the primary lever for a short sleeper. All
fixed; two generalizable rules appended to COACHING-PRINCIPLES.md (2026-08-17).
