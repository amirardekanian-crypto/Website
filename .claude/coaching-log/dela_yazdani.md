## Cycle 02 — Structural Build · 2026-08-14 · RETURNING
(first logged cycle — Cycle 1 predates the log)

**The read** — C1 was designed as 4 weeks and ran 17. It produced real adaptation for about six
weeks (seated leg curl 23→36, leg press 100→140 lb, kickback 27→36) then went flat everywhere, and
two lifts went backwards. Mechanism: every exercise prescribed at RPE 6 with no dose progression
written anywhere. She hit RPE 6 honestly on the same weight for three months — she did what the card
said; the card never told her to move. Confirming tell: the lifts that DID progress are all
pin-loaded machines where the next increment is obvious. The free-weight lifts, where she'd have to
make the judgement call herself, are the ones that stalled. Coaching gap, not motivation gap.

Recovery: the intake picture (sleep 4/10, stress 9/10) that justified RPE 6 is four months stale.
Last five sessions run 4.0–4.25 composite, sleep 4/5, stress 4/5, 7.5h most nights. She also
volunteers 30 min of incline treadmill unprompted. The recovery constraint is gone; the real
constraint is ATTENDANCE — 21 sessions in 17 weeks, clustered, 9–13 day gaps, never more than 3 in a
calendar week. Hence 3 days.

Injuries: none live. The "left leg has pain" note on the RDL was confirmed by Amir as a stale
carried-forward log artifact — dropped, no modification. The left-arm complaint (4 unprompted
reports, no pain word ever used) is a strength asymmetry, and it is specific to the MACHINE chest
press: one shared stack lets the right side prop up the left until the left quits and the set ends
(14/14/9, then 9/9/5). Answered with independent-load pressing + a unilateral row led by the weak side.

**Decisions** — PROGRESS: Hip Thrust, DB RDL, Machine Leg Press all 15 reps @RPE6 → 8-10 @RPE8;
Machine Lying Leg Curl kept as the one retained accessory (only hamstring machine with logged working
weights, so it carries the recalibration on known ground) with RPE 6→8 + controlled eccentric.
REPLACE: Machine Chest Press→Incline DB Press · Goblet Squat + DB Reverse Lunge→DB Split Squat ·
Cable Seated Row→Chest-Supported DB Row (was duplicated D2+D3 in C1, she flagged it) · Lat Pulldown
→neutral-grip chip · Standing→Seated DB OHP (her request, 30 May, never actioned) · Arnold Press,
Chest Fly, Bicep Curl, Tricep Pushdown, Seated Leg Curl, Sumo Squat, Glute Kickback (stack max),
Russian Twist/Dead Bug/Bird Dog all rotated. ADD: Incline Treadmill Walk (her own work, written in
with a session-indexed ladder), Seated Machine Hip Abduction, Machine Leg Extension, Cable Lateral
Raise, Cable Pull Through, Stability Ball Leg Curl, Cable Pallof Press. 25% carryover.

Amir's calls at the checkpoint: 3 days · RDL pain note is stale · no period protocol · core stays
light at 3 sets · cardio on Days 2+3 rather than after leg day.

Post-audit corrections: bounded the two open-ended load ramps; staged the RPE 6→8 jump into the
notes rather than onto the cards (house rule: the card carries her normal target); cut Day 1 from 8
to 7 eccentric hamstring sets; raised cardio 25→30 min to match her actual logs; rebuilt the ladder
to move one variable per step and indexed it to sessions; removed Standing Hip Circle (banned
joint-circle drill, had been in C1 too); moved Leg Extension to D1 / Hip Abduction to D3 so no
lower-body muscle depends on the day she drops; changed the RDL fallback off 45° Back Extension
(riskier for a novice than the lift it covers) and the split squat fallback off Reverse Lunge (a
lateral swap, not a regression).

Correction at assembly: the design had Incline Treadmill Walk on BOTH Day 2 and Day 3 as a stated
exception to one-exercise-once-per-cycle. That is not survivable in this app — `setLogKey()` in
`program.html` keys every exercise log by NAME alone with no day component, so two identically-named
exercises share one log array including the done tick. Ticking the walk on Day 2 would have rendered
Day 3's walk already complete. (This is the same mechanism that made C1's duplicated Cable Seated Row
carry its weight across days, which she noticed and wrote about.) Resolved by giving Day 3 an Assault
Bike at 20 min instead — different name, no collision, same easy steady-state effort, and its Coach's
Note permits any bike or the treadmill if the machine is busy. The laddered treadmill walk stays on
Day 2. **Any future cycle must treat one-exercise-once-per-cycle as a technical constraint, not a
stylistic rule that can be waived with a stated exception.**
