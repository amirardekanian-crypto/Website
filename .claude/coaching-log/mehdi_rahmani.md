## Cycle 01 — Bedrock · 2026-08-01 · NEW

**The read** — Occupational athlete: tennis player/coach + padel, 7-8 h/day on court,
year-round, no off-season. Recovery ceiling set FIRST: sleep excellent (10/10, 7-8h) but
the adaptive budget is pre-spent by ~50 h/wk of court work + inconsistent nutrition →
weekly hard sets deliberately under the hypertrophy band (quads 6-8, hams/glutes 8-10,
back/scap ~9-12, press 4-6 — the under-dose is the arc's stated logic, not an error).
Arc logic (locked at roadmap, 4-lens panel-derived, judged 8.5/10 to the winning
recovery-constrained-pragmatist lens): the gym is the counterweight to court life, not
the engine — intensity is the only axis 3×45 min can progress; heaviness delivered in
tendon-smart order (calibrate → tendon bank → fives → triples → keeper). No gym plyo
ever this arc (court IS the elastic dose). No injuries current/past; risks are
occupational — lateral elbow, cuff, patellar, Achilles, the four sites racket
professionals actually break. Court load even across the week → gym days spaced evenly,
all three days full-body and self-contained so the bad-week protocol (2 sessions = full
compliance) actually works. First cycle with this coach: straight sets only, primaries
open at RPE 6 (no baselines exist), progression coach-driven weekly from logs.

**Decisions** — PRIMARY LIFT SELECTIONS (arc anchors, locked at the roadmap checkpoint,
carried through all 5 cycles of the arc):
  Squat  → Barbell Back Squat (transfer + his explicit "lift heavy" override SFR;
           Leg Press deferred, stated trade-off)
  Hinge  → Barbell Romanian Deadlift (posterior bias per tennis rule, knee-sparing,
           pre-fits Cycle 2's HSR work)
  Pull   → Chest-Supported Dumbbell Row (scap health, zero lower-back tax — chosen over
           weighted pull-up as the tracked anchor)
  S-leg  → Dumbbell Bulgarian Split Squat (loadable, straight sets, never superset)

Amir's checkpoint calls: court load roughly even all week → gym days spaced evenly
(no asymmetric placement needed); protein habit anchor added IN from day one (engage
wrote the note rather than deferring to C2); all four anchors approved unchanged.

Adversarial verification panel (3 auditors — clinical, house-rules lint, dose/time)
applied before this shipped: autoregulation reworded with a floor-6 clause (the
locked arc's "-1 RPE" rule would otherwise print an unselectable RPE 5 on every
RPE-6 accessory); Day 2 cut to fit the 45-min cap (wrist-extension iso consolidated
to one weekly slot on Day 3 instead of appearing on two days, RDL rest trimmed
150s→120s); weekly press brought up to the locked 4-6 band (was 2, now 5 across two
days) via a Landmine Press addition on Day 2; primaries represcribed to open at RPE 6
instead of a flat 7, so the stated calibration ramp is actually what prints in the app;
straps prescribed on the RDL from week 2 (grip is never meant to be the limiter — court
already trains his grip, and cumulative gripping volume across the week risked masking
true hamstring RPE); the Achilles calfraise moved from dumbbells-in-hand to a grip-free
machine hold for the same reason; Achilles frequency doubled to 2×/week with a genuine
soleus-bias variant (bent-knee) added on Day 3, matching patellar's frequency; Wall Sit
loaded with a plate from day one rather than bodyweight (bodyweight alone under-doses a
trained 28-year-old — it reads as an endurance burn, not a tendon-loading stimulus);
week-1 eccentric tempos on Squat and RDL softened to 2s (from the arc's target 3s) as a
DOMS guard — his income depends on moving well on court the morning after a session, and
this novel a stimulus in week 1 reliably produces 24-72h soreness in the unadapted;
Spanish Squat (the panel's second patellar pick) deferred out of Cycle 1 entirely — the
full 8-exposure tendon-armour plan the panel wanted simply doesn't fit inside 3×45 min in
an install month, so each site gets one quality weekly exposure plus daily court load
this cycle, and Cycle 2 (Tendon Bank) is where frequency doubles across all four sites
by design, not by oversight.
