## Cycle 04 — Power Reload · 2026-08-24 · RETURNING
*(first logged cycle — cycles 1–3 predate the log)*

**The read**

*Adaptation.* C3 produced no primary progression. Back Squat sat at one logged load for ten
weeks while RPE drifted 7 → 8 → a 10 on the final top set, after which she dropped the weight
herself. Trap Bar went 30 (C2) → 25 → 20 kg. Only the dumbbell lifts moved — reverse lunge
+50%, B-stance RDL +25%, landmine push press +100% — i.e. everything where a 2.5 kg jump is
available. The root cause is not effort, it is prescription: 4×5 at RPE 8 with a 3s eccentric
is a load ceiling, not a progression. She hit the effort target long before the bar was heavy
enough to drive adaptation. Every barbell lift in C4 therefore drops reps and loses the slow
eccentric so the same RPE lands on a heavier bar.

*Recovery & lifestyle.* Readiness 2.83/5 (C2: 3.08), sleep 2.67/5, sleep rated ≤2 in 4 of 9
rated sessions. Session RPE rose 6.87 → 7.92 on static loads. This is life load, not training
load — the same separation flagged in her C2 review. Consequence: three days, ~55-min cap
(down from C3's ~70), autoregulation protocol carried forward unchanged, and sleep gets its
own notes card as the cycle's named limiter.

*Adherence.* 13 of 20 sessions (65%), stretched over 8.5 weeks by a 26-day layoff
(6 Jul → 1 Aug) whose cause is still unconfirmed. Day 4 attempted zero times in the entire
cycle. Partials rose to 46% of sessions. The return was strong — 5 sessions in 12 days,
including her best week since the opener — but loads had detrained and the first session back
was 1,278 AU (RPE 9 × 142 min), roughly 2× her cycle average and 2× the designed session
length. Frequency is the binding constraint, so C4 is designed at three days rather than four
and the conditioning moves inside an attended day. The behavioural read is consistent and
legible: she protects warm-ups, jumps, primaries and rehab work; she sheds core, carries, and
anything that is not strength. C4 removes the two things she reliably drops rather than
re-prescribing them and hoping.

*Injury.* Knees (bilateral 5/10 at the C2 opener) and both shoulders (R 6/10, L 5/10) —
zero reports across all 13 sessions of C3, under the exact loads meant to test them. Reactive
circuit completed 6/6, shoulder integrity block 4/4 never once skipped (including on the two
RPE-9 days), landmine press doubled. **Amir confirmed 2026-08-19: both resolved, no current
injuries.** Reactive and overhead work are fully re-earned. Closed with her in writing on the
wins card rather than allowed to silently vanish.

*NEW standing item — from Amir 2026-08-19, invisible in every session log: **upper back pain
when she plays a lot.*** It never surfaces in a session report because it is driven by court
volume, not gym work, which is exactly why it had to come from the coach rather than the data.
This is now the main programming input for the upper day, and it **inverted the first draft**:
that draft graduated the shoulder-integrity block DOWN (3 exercises → 2) on the strength of the
resolved shoulder, and signed back/lat volume off at 7 sets/wk as "maintenance by design."
Wrong read. Scapular and thoracic endurance under playing volume is a *do more* problem, not a
*do less* one. Posterior upper-body volume goes UP instead: pull work 7 → 14 sets/wk, press
held at 7 (a deliberate 1:2 press:pull, per the standing posture/tennis principle), Cable Face
Pull re-earned from C2 as the best-fit movement, Prone Y Raise for lower trap, Thoracic
Windmill into prep. The conditioning choice follows from the same read — see Decisions.

*Capacity.* Hold total volume, redistribute it. Four thin days become three dense ones.

*Roadmap.* **DEVIATION.** The locked roadmap had C4 = Performance Peak · "Sharpen the Weapon."
There is no peak to sharpen: she never actually received the power block (13/20 sessions, zero
conditioning, no primary progression, finished mid-detrain). C4 re-runs the power intent with a
working progression scheme plus the upper-back build. Performance Peak shifts intact to C5 and
Armor & Maintain to C6 — the plan is delayed by one block, not rewritten.
