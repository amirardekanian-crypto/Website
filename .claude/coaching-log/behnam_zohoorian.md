## Cycle 01 — Bedrock · 2026-08-02 · NEW

**The read** — Recovery inputs elite (sleep 10/10 at 7–8h, stress 1/10) but three things cap
the gym ceiling anyway: age 16 (tendon/spine/growth-plate timelines don't speed up with
sleep), average/inconsistent nutrition, and 5–6 padel days running concurrently (9–10 total
weekly sessions). C1 ceiling set at ~8–10 cost-weighted sets/lower-body muscle/week at
RPE 6–7 — a skill-acquisition dose, not a growth dose; novice physiology grows on it
regardless. Bodyweight-only history (pull-ups/dips only reference points) = true novice to
external load ("trust the bar over the résumé"), so pedagogy leads all selection; transfer
explicitly deferred per locked roadmap. Old lower-back + shoulder = awareness flags only
(intake form said "None"; Amir confirmed old/resolved — a side-channel note, not the form).
Chronic-back deferral rules judged NOT triggered; hinge still taught brace-first with
trunk-supported fallback as a hedge. Overhead pressing ~zero (smash volume already high),
posterior-bias upper 9:6 pull:push. Structure: 3 full-body days over Lower/Upper/Lower —
three motor-learning exposures per pattern-family per week at moderate per-day leg stress;
no single day can wreck his court week. Undulation moderate / low-moderate / moderate; no
peak day exists in Bedrock by design. No supersets (first cycle). RPE ceiling 7 with two
stated exceptions (the session-1 baselines, technical-failure AMRAPs). Adversarial panel
(clinical · house-rules · dose/time) ran per standing order: caught the baseline-AMRAP vs
RPE-ceiling contradiction (all 3 auditors independently), the box-jump dismount gap, the
missing double-day rule, Copenhagen intro dose, back-extension effort cap, and 3 redundant
intent chips — all applied. Time math independently verified: D1 ~53.5' / D2 ~49' /
D3 ~51' vs 60' cap; D1 carries a time valve (skip Standing Machine Calf Raise past ~50').
