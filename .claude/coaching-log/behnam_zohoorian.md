weekly sessions). C1 ceiling set at ~8–10 cost-weighted sets/lower-body muscle/week at
RPE 6–7 — a skill-acquisition dose, not a growth dose; novice physiology grows on it
regardless. Bodyweight-only history (pull-ups/dips only reference points) = true novice to
external load ("trust the bar over the résumé"), so pedagogy leads all selection; transfer
explicitly deferred per locked roadmap. Old lower-back + shoulder = awareness flags only

Chronic-back deferral rules judged NOT triggered; hinge still taught brace-first with
trunk-supported fallback as a hedge. Overhead pressing ~zero (smash volume already high),

only 2 exposures each on the RDL and split squat, neither load ever moved, and both are still
sitting where they started (7.5 kg/hand, 10 kg). Goblet squat did move properly, 8 → 14 kg.

⚠️ **Three C1 reads were WRONG in the first pass of this review and Amir corrected them** —
recorded here so the next cycle doesn't re-derive the same errors. (1) The **film gate did NOT
fail**: Behnam sent every video to Amir directly and Amir cleared them. Videos go to Amir, not
into any system this pipeline can see — never infer "no videos" from the absence of data.
(2) **The AMRAP baselines DO exist**: 13 strict pull-ups and 31 push-ups, held by Amir, just
never typed into the app log. Still no DIP number. (3) The **RPE 10s and the uniform 5/5
readiness were a logging mistake**, not a fatigue or grinding signal — all C1 RPE and readiness
data is noise and nothing in this cycle is designed against it. Loads are the only trustworthy
column in that log.

**Capacity: UP.** Recovery inputs stay elite (sleep 10/10 at 7–8h, stress 1/10), form is cleared
on all three primaries, and 13 pull-ups at 70 kg says the upper body has far more capacity than
C1's 3×6 pull-ups and 3×12 push-ups ever touched — that was maintenance, not training. C1's
~8–10 cost-weighted sets/lower muscle/week skill-dose moves to ~11–14 with real intensity.

**Amir's brief for this cycle, verbatim:** *"he wants to be able to play lower to the ground for
longer when he is defending at the back of the court, so lets practice those positions and a bit
leg endurance… we need to have plyometric as much as we can, and lets add medicine ball throws."*

**The translation that shaped the whole cycle:** "stay low longer" is NOT an endurance problem.
It is **strength at deep hip/knee flexion plus ankle range plus adductor capacity** — strength is
joint-angle specific and he has never loaded that position. So the ask is delivered by making the
strength work **deep and paused** (1s bottom pause on squat and split squat, front foot elevated
for range, Cossack for low-and-wide, Wall Sit as the literal position held) rather than by adding
an endurance block. That is what let Amir's asks and the locked roadmap's "First Iron" strength
identity coexist instead of competing.

**Plyos were capped deliberately against the "as much as we can" ask.** 84 high-intensity ground
contacts (from ~45), five distinct qualities, every set max intent with full rest — *presence*
increased, *volume* controlled. 16 y/o, open growth plates, 5–6 court days, inconsistent
nutrition: that is the standard profile for patellar/achilles/apophyseal trouble, and padel is
already thousands of low-amplitude contacts. Med ball throws are the free win — near-zero
lower-limb cost, and rotational power was a total gap.

**Roadmap deviation: the 4th day was CUT** (Amir, this cycle: *"go with 3 days, you can make days
longer if you want"*). The roadmap's C2 called for a 4th upper day carrying the weighted chin-up
and dip work. That content folded into the 3 days instead and sessions went from ~60 to ~65–68
min. Weighted pull-up work is live; dip loading is deferred to week 2 pending its baseline.

**Adversarial panel** (clinical · house-rules · dose/time) per standing order — it earned its
keep, 10 clinical must-fixes. The four that changed real programming:
1. **Single-Leg Lateral Hop cut entirely.** Drafted 3×5 e/s *behind* 4×4 e/s Lateral Bound — the
   most advanced plyo in the cycle, entering with no unilateral base, landing on a pre-fatigued
   leg. The rung order is bilateral → unilateral stick → unilateral reactive; the middle rung is
   this whole cycle's job. Deferred to C3.
2. **The plyo budget was undercounted ~9×.** 3 min of jump rope on 3 days is ~900–1,000 contacts
   that the "114 contacts" figure ignored. Rope cut to 2 min; **D3 swapped to Assault Bike** so
   there is no impact before max-intent bounds; budget now states high- and low-intensity
   contacts separately.
3. **Groin was loaded from four directions at once** — Copenhagen 15→25s, a new end-range paused
   Cossack, 62 lateral contacts, plus court. At 16 the pubic apophysis is open, so the failure
   mode is apophysitis/avulsion, not a strain. Now: Copenhagen 15→**18s** only, Cossack starts
   bodyweight/half-depth/no pause, both get fallbacks, and a groin rule ships in the notes.
4. **The overhead-zero rule was being defeated by my own programming.** Medicine Ball Overhead
   Throw is a max-intent smash-arc action — *more* shoulder-demanding than the pressing the rule
   bans. Replaced with Chest Pass.
Also applied: **hamstrings were under-dosed and hidden by lumping** (Cossack is adductor/quad,
FFE split squat is quad-biased with no knee flexion → real direct hamstring work was 4 sets, in
the cycle that doubles jumping) → **Stability Ball Leg Curl added**; D2 prep cued lumbar flexion
and hamstring end-range immediately before the first barbell RDL → **Inchworm swapped for Dowel
Hip Hinge**; D3 had zero ankle prep before its highest ankle demand → **Ankle Dorsiflexion Rocks
added**; Band Pass-Through cut (it cued end-range anterior shoulder stretch on a flagged shoulder,
in the one session with no upper work); Wall Sit's avoid cue told a 16-year-old to hold through
"hurt"; several tempo/intent contradictions.

**Three panel findings OVERRULED, with reasons** (so they are not re-litigated next cycle):
- *"Reverse the double-day rule to gym-before-padel."* Rejected — it contradicts Amir's own
  codified principle (2026-08-02, written from this athlete's C1 audit) protecting the court
  session. Substance accepted a different way: gym still follows padel, but **the power block is
  what gets cut on a double day, not the primary.** Same risk removed, house rule intact.
- *"Stagger the two barbell lifts across weeks."* Rejected — that is a mid-cycle prescription
  change, which the house rules forbid on the card. Replaced with a **week-1 RPE 6 ceiling on

- *"Cut jump rope."* Partially — 2 min, and off D3 entirely.
