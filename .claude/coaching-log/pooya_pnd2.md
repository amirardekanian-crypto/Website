**Post-ship correction (2026-07-18)** — Amir caught, mid-cycle, that every superset pair across all 4 days had been built as two separate `standard` exercises each tagged with a decorative `superset` chip, instead of the app's real superset format (`type: circuit`, one shared name + rest, both exercises in `items[]`). Practical effect: no visual grouping showed which exercises were paired, and each exercise ran its own independent rest timer instead of alternating into a shared rest per round — the opposite of what a "no dead time" density superset is for. Converted all pairs to circuit blocks with no change to sets/reps/RPE/exercise selection: Day 1 → Push-Pull Superset (DB Bench Press + Chest-Supported DB Row) + Chest-Rear Delt Superset (Cable Chest Press + Prone DB Reverse Fly), Day 2 → Hamstring-Calf Superset (Stability Ball Leg Curl + Seated Machine Calf Raise), Day 3 → Chest-Back Superset (Cable Chest Fly + Single-Arm Cable Row) + Arm Superset (Dumbbell Hammer Curl + Cable Tricep Pushdown) — previously 4 exercises bundled in one block with no distinction between the two separate pairs — Day 4 → Quad-Hamstring Superset (Machine Leg Extension + Machine Lying Leg Curl). Root cause traced to the pipeline docs themselves: SCHEMA.md's chip-example tables, the /program-design SPEC template, and COACHING-PRINCIPLES.md's "Chips & modifiers" section all listed `superset` as a valid chip/intent value, contradicting the correct rule (superset = circuit) that existed elsewhere in the same files — all three fixed the same day so this doesn't recur on another athlete.

**In-cycle edit (2026-07-18)** — Amir flagged that conditioning-circuit exercise selection had gone stale across athletes (Kettlebell Swing / Medicine Ball Slam / Farmer's Walk on repeat, cycle after cycle). ## Cycle 04 — Strength Reclaim · 2026-08-19 · RETURNING

**The read** — C3 was a strong strength cycle and a failed metabolic one. Adherence 15/20 (75%) across five complete weeks, but the day distribution did the damage: D1 ×5, D2 ×4, D3 ×4, **D4 ×2**. Since the posterior chain and the conditioning both lived on D4, hamstrings/glutes delivered only 5.2 of 10 programmed sets/wk (52%) and quads 6.8 of 10 (68%), while chest/delts/back landed 87–94%. Conditioning delivered ~40% of dose: the Conditioning Circuit ran once complete (Jul 19, ×4 @RPE 8–9) and was skipped Aug 9 ("dying to the heat"); Bike Intervals ran 3/5, one truncated to ~6 min, one skipped. **A metabolic cycle executed as a lifting cycle.** Strength response was the best on record: Lat Pulldown 43→52 kg (+21%, e1RM 60.2→72.8) — the neutral-grip −10% reset broke a six-week plateau and the wk-3 swap trigger never fired; Machine Leg Press 64→78 (+22%, clean +5 kg/session, **zero knee complaints all cycle** — the BSS→Leg Press swap fully vindicated); Machine Shoulder Press 34→41 (+21%); Hack Squat 35→50 (+43%) but with a real miss — he sat on 35 kg for three weeks at RPE 7 (target 7–8; the wk-1 job was to validate 40) then jumped +43% in one step, so roughly three weeks of under-loading the primary quad lift. **The clean structural finding: every fine-increment cable/stack lift progressed (7 of 7); every fixed-dumbbell lift stalled (6 of 6)** — CS DB Row 16 kg across 5 sessions, Prone Rev Fly 6 kg, Cable Lat Raise 18, Cable Crunch 36, Pallof 23. The density lever ("all sets ×12 @≤8 → +2 kg/DB") fired exactly once. Cause is increment size, not motivation: 6→8 kg on a reverse fly is +33%, unreachable inside an RPE 7 window. Enzymes **never re-tested** (5.5 weeks overdue), so the ≤60 min / no-maximal-grind constraint stands and the protein problem stays unresolved. **Knee confirmed clear by Amir** — the staged return advances to rung 2. Capacity: hold set count, raise intensity, cut total volume. The roadmap's Strength Reclaim fits as locked, and he is now genuinely in the position it was designed for — just heavier than the roadmap assumed, not lighter.
