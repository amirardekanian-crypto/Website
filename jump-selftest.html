Jump engine self test

Builds jumps in memory where the true height is known exactly, runs them through the real tracker and the real formulas, and reports the error. Run this after changing anything under assets/js/jump/.

Takes a few seconds. It decodes nothing, it only exercises the tracking and the maths, which are the parts that can be wrong without looking wrong.

Not linked from the site and marked noindex. Cases live in assets/js/jump/selftest.js.
