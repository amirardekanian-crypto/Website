# The "Start here" video — the short one that goes with the login WhatsApp

**Status: PLAN for Amir's approval · 2026-10-11.** Nothing is recorded, generated or spent yet.
**Farsi first** (Amir, 2026-10-11), **AI voice**, English version later from the same pictures.

## What it is

A video of about 90 seconds that arrives in the same WhatsApp conversation as the login message (the one with the link, username and password). It
teaches one thing: how to get in and do the first session. Everything else (Home and Game Plan in depth, Guided, Records, Body Weight, the
Library) belongs to the longer chaptered video that comes after this one.

| Decision | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | **Vertical, 1080 × 1920 MP4** | It is watched on the phone it arrives on, and WhatsApp plays vertical full-screen. |
| How it is sent | **The file itself, on WhatsApp** (not a YouTube link) | YouTube is hard to reach in Iran; a file always plays. The longer video can still go on YouTube. |
| Pictures | **The real app**, recaptured sharp, with Farsi callouts | The viewer sees exactly the screens they will meet. Costs no credits. |
| Voice | **AI voice, Farsi** | Amir's call. Cheap: a few credits. Quality in Farsi is unproven until we hear it (see *Voice*). |
| Words on screen | **Farsi**, Vazirmatn, Persian numerals, no letter-spacing | The Farsi-first rule for all new content. The app's own labels stay English, because the app is English. |
| Sound | Voice only, plus very small tap sounds | Higgsfield makes no music. Voice-only reads cleanly on a phone speaker. |

No picture or clip is generated: the pictures are the real app. So the voice is the only spend.

## How it is made (nothing here spends credits)

1. **Capture** the screens from the real app at high resolution (the test athlete, an invented name), one short sequence per beat: the tap, the typing,
   the strip opening, so it moves like the real thing.
2. **Build** one animated page that is a pure function of time (your timeline-kit idea): the phone, the taps, the callouts, the light on each control.
   Farsi in Vazirmatn, brand green, **orange (clay) the only accent, no yellow**. The callouts follow your **reading rule** (0.8 s plus 0.25 s a word)
   and are checked by a script before any render.
3. **Time it to the voice:** the picture is timed from the real audio, not from my estimate.
4. **Render** here to a 1080 × 1920 MP4 (H.264, AAC), about 12 minutes of compute, then check frames and sound.

**You get:** the MP4 (small enough for WhatsApp, about 15–25 MB), a cover frame, and the source so it can be redone when a screen changes (change a label,
re-render; re-record only the line that changed).
