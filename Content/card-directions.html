One thing worth deciding with them

Directions 1, 3 and 4 all show live numbers on the card. The Ceiling already has them. The habit card does not — right now this app deliberately holds no habit state, no XP maths and no level formula, because duplicating those would be a third copy to keep in sync. Showing a level or a streak here means reading AA Proof's own stored keys, the same read-only way The Ceiling already borrows body weight. That is doable and cheap, but it is a real decision, not a cosmetic one — so if you pick 1, 3 or 4, say whether the habit card should carry a live number or just a line of copy.
