Every direction stays inside the rules. Green #0E4A36, clay #C7552F as the only accent (clay-2 #E06B43 on dark), paper #FAF7F2, Barlow Condensed for display, Barlow for UI, Space Mono for the small caps. No yellow anywhere. House ease on every transition.

And inside the speed work. Inputs are 16px so iOS can’t zoom the page on focus, the autocomplete names are untouched so your password manager still fills and offers to save, the password-reveal eye you shipped this afternoon is in all three, and the markup stays plain HTML in the body — which is what lets the login paint in ~47ms instead of waiting on the network. Directions 02 and 03 add no image at all; Direction 01 adds one 34KB court photo that the service worker now caches after the first open.
