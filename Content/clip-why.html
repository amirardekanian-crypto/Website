Why This Cycle Matters

This is where the body starts genuinely changing. Foundation Forge taught your nervous system how to move; this cycle teaches it how to produce force.

Every strength lift now has a concrete rep and tempo prescription. You'll feel weights get heavier and progress become visible session by session. The goal is not to chase fatigue — it is to chase clean, heavy reps at the tempos written.

Jumps and throws come first in every session, while you're fresh. Treat the bounds and med-ball throws as tennis practice, not conditioning — every rep is maximum intent, then full recovery.

Expected outcomes after this cycle:
✓
Measurable strength gains in the back squat and Romanian deadlift
✓
Sharper, more controlled first step and change of direction
✓
Confident, pain-free overhead pressing and serving
✓
Rotational power that shows up in your groundstrokes

Cycle 3 is where the weights get lighter and the movements get faster. You've built the platform; now we express it.

Expect more contrast work: a heavy squat paired with a jump, a heavy press paired with a med-ball throw. The strength carries over — but only if you chase speed of execution, not load.

This is the cycle where on-court performance starts to visibly change.
