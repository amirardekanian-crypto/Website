The idea in plain words

The reel opens on an empty clay court at first light. A tennis ball drops onto the white line and bounces in a puff of dust. Then the number 16 counts up: 16 weeks to a tennis body.

We walk through the four blocks as if they were one day, from a gym at dawn to a court under floodlights. One tennis ball bounces along the whole reel, and it bounces higher in every block. That is the progress.

Then a phone rises and shows the real app. The reel ends on the same court at night, with one button.

The reel, beat by beat

Seven scenes, 30 seconds. These are stills from the finished reel. The Farsi lines are drafts, so change any word you like. All text stays away from the top and the bottom of the screen, where Instagram puts its own buttons.

0 – 5 s

Opening. A clay ball drops onto the white baseline and bounces in a puff of dust. The 16 counts up while the 16 week marks light along the line. Then the line lands: 16 weeks to a tennis body.

5 – 8 s

Block 1, Build. The picture wipes in through a moving clay line. The ball hops to week 4 and lights the marks it passes.

8 – 11 s

Block 2, Develop. The dawn court starts to fade toward night. The ball hops higher, to week 8.

11 – 14 s

Block 3, Express. Higher again, to week 12. Dust and a sweep of light cross the picture.

14 – 17 s

Block 4, Transfer. The biggest hop, to week 16. The floodlights come on.

17 – 25 s

The app. The ball rolls off and the phone rises. Four real screens swap, each with a tap: a session, the step-by-step player, a test (the coin pings) and a lesson. The counters 16, 114, 7 and 22 run as each one opens. Last, offline mode.

25 – 30 s

The button. The night court. The ball drops on the button, the button gives and a ripple runs out. "Link in bio" and the "buy once" line arrive after. The words copy the button on your links page.
