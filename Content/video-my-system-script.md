# "How I Coach" — to-camera video script

Amir facing the camera, explaining the whole system. Roughly 7 minutes at a normal
speaking pace. Source of truth: `Content/HOW-IT-WORKS.md` — every claim here is in
that doc, nothing invented.

**Shoot it in 8 takes, one per section.** Easier to nail, easier to cut, and you can
drop a section later without reshooting anything.

**If you want a 3 to 4 minute version:** cut section 7 (the Library and Playbook) and
trim section 3 down to just the goal-conflict part. Everything else stays.

---

## 1 — OPEN (about 25 seconds)

If you've ever downloaded a training plan and stopped after three weeks, it wasn't
because you were lazy. It was because nobody was watching.

I want to walk you through exactly how I coach. Step by step. So before you spend a
pound with me, you know exactly what you're getting.

First thing you should know. This is not a PDF. You're not buying a file. You're
hiring a coach. Your programme lives in your own private app, and it gets adjusted
every week based on how you're actually responding.

And one rule runs through all of it. Nothing without a reason. Every exercise, every
set, every rep range is chosen on purpose, and I'll always tell you what that purpose
is.

---

## 2 — STEP ONE: I ASK FIRST (about 60 seconds)

Step one. I get to know you.

Before I choose a single exercise, you fill in a proper intake form.

I ask about your goals, and the one specific thing you most want to achieve. If you
play tennis or padel, I ask where you play and how often, because your court time is
training load too.

I ask about your training history, and your current best lifts if you know them, so I
know your real starting point.

I ask what hurts. Current pain, old injuries, surgeries that still affect you, and
anything you've been told not to do.

I ask about your recovery. How you sleep, how much stress you're under, how you eat,
how heavy your week is outside the gym.

And then the practical stuff. How many days a week you can genuinely train, how long
each session can be, and exactly what equipment you can get to.

I ask all of that for one reason. A great programme you can't actually do is a bad
programme. Your real life is a design input. It's not an excuse.

## 3 — STEP TWO: I ANALYSE BEFORE I PRESCRIBE (about 75 seconds)

Step two. I analyse before I prescribe. Information on its own isn't a plan. This is
where the coaching actually starts.

First, I line your goals up. Some goals stack together nicely. Some are fighting each
other for the same recovery.

So if you tell me you want to get noticeably stronger, more powerful, and bulletproof
your joints, on two thirty minute sessions a week, I'm going to be honest with you
about what that realistically buys you. Either we find you a bit more time, or we pick
the goal that matters most right now and we sequence the rest. I'd rather tell you the
truth up front than promise you everything and deliver none of it.

Second, I respect the order your body demands. Some things gate other things. If your
knee hurts, I'm not chasing top end speed through it. We calm the knee down first,
then we build the speed on a foundation that won't break. Health and capacity come
before performance. Every time.

And if you're an athlete, I run a proper needs analysis for your sport. For tennis and
padel, I look at what the game actually asks of your body. The sprints and the changes
of direction. The rotational power behind every shot. The shoulder and hip health that
keeps you on court. The repeat effort endurance to still be sharp in the third set.

Then I work backwards from that. Your programme is built to transfer to the court. Not
to look good in the gym.

---

## 4 — STEP THREE: I BUILD YOUR SIX MONTHS (about 80 seconds)

Step three. I build your six months.

Your programme is a journey, not a workout. It's a chain of cycles, roughly five weeks
each, and every cycle is a mission. It has its own focus, its own reason for existing,
and a clear outcome you should walk away with.

One cycle lays your foundation. The next builds raw strength. The next turns that
strength into power. The next armours you against injury.

And I plan all six months up front. So you can see what you're doing each month, what
you'll have achieved by the end of it, and why that step has to come before the next
one.

On exercise choice. For most goals I pick the movements with the highest payoff for
the lowest cost. The ones that build the most without burying you in fatigue or risk.
Sometimes with athletes I'll pick a harder movement on purpose, because it transfers
better to your sport. When I do that, I'll tell you why.

Now here's the part most plans ignore completely. Recovery sets the ceiling.

Your ability to recover decides how much training you can actually turn into progress.
That's why I asked about your sleep, your stress and your week. If your life is heavy
right now, I don't just pile on more volume and hope. I shape the week properly, so
the hard days are genuinely hard and the easy days let you absorb the work. Your
programme fits your recovery. Not somebody else's ideal.

And every prescription is specific. Sets, reps, target effort, tempo, rest. You always
know exactly what the session is asking of you.

[B-roll: a roadmap on screen, cycle names in order]

## 5 — STEP FOUR: YOU TRAIN, THROUGH YOUR OWN APP (about 70 seconds)

Step four. You train, through your own app.

When you start, you get a private link, and it installs on your phone like any other
app. That's where your programme lives.

Before each session there's a quick wellbeing check. You rate your sleep, your energy,
your soreness, your stress, and how you feel overall. It takes seconds. And it does
two things. It lets the session adapt to how you actually walked in that day, and it
shows me exactly how you're holding up.

During the session, everything you need is on the card. Tap any exercise and you get a
demo video, the full prescription, and coaching cues. What to do, what to feel, and
the one mistake to avoid. You're never standing in the gym guessing what an exercise
is or how it's supposed to look.

As you go, you log your work. Each set, you put in the weight, your effort, and tick
it off. There's a session timer and a rest timer to keep you moving. At the end you
rate the session, leave me a note if you want, and send it.

That log isn't busywork. It's the raw material I use to make your next week better.

[B-roll: screen record the app. Wellbeing check, an exercise card, logging a set]

## 6 — STEP FIVE: I'M WATCHING (about 70 seconds)

Step five. And this is the half that makes it coaching and not just an app.

I watch your data. Everything you log comes through to a private coach dashboard. Your
progress, your adherence, your readiness trend, your training load. It flags the early
warning signs for me. A drop in readiness. A spike in load. A quiet week. So problems
get caught before they turn into injuries or burnout.

The promise behind all of this is simple. Before you get hurt, you get a message from
me.

You also get a real weekly check in. Every week we go through how you're doing. Your
sleep, your energy, your soreness, how you're responding, your wins, your niggles, and
what you want next. On a call or on WhatsApp. It's a relationship, not a file drop.

And your plan keeps changing as you change. If a movement doesn't suit you, or it
hurts, or you just can't do it, we swap it and I write down why, so it doesn't come
back unless it's genuinely right for you. At the end of every cycle I read all of that
together to decide what the next cycle should do. You're never running a stale plan.

And I'm always one message away inside the app.

[B-roll: the coach dashboard, a readiness trend line]

## 7 — WHAT ELSE YOU GET (about 45 seconds) — CUT THIS FOR THE SHORT VERSION

Two more things on top of your plan.

The app has a Library, and it has two sides.

One side is Sessions. Ready to run workouts, drills and resets you can do on your own
whenever you want a bit extra. A travel day. A busy week. An active recovery day. Or
just because you feel like moving. They're a bonus, not homework.

The other side is the Tennis Playbook. That's everything a tennis player or coach
needs to get better on court. How to prepare your body and your head for match day.
What actually helps you recover and what's a myth. The mental game. Nutrition and
supplements, what's worth your money and what isn't. And deeper material for the
coaches who want the reasoning, not just the drill.

Same evidence based thinking that's behind your programme. Opened up so you can keep
learning between sessions.

---

## 8 — WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT, AND HOW TO START (about 45 seconds)

So, why is this different.

It's built for you, not pulled off a shelf. Nothing in it is random, and you'll always
know the reason. A coach is actually watching, every single week. It adapts as you
adapt, and that's the whole point. It's a real app, not a document.

And it comes from sport science, not from what happened to work for me. I've got an
MSc in Strength and Conditioning and an MSc in Applied Exercise Physiology, and I've
coached over a thousand tennis and padel players.

If that's what you've been looking for, the intake form takes about five minutes. I
read every application myself, and I'll get back to you so we can talk about whether
this is actually right for you.

Link's in my bio.

Move better. Hit harder. Last longer.

## Delivery notes

- Look down the lens, not at yourself on the screen.
- Say the first line of each section before you feel ready. The pause before a take is
  what makes an opening sound stiff.
- Land these four lines. Slow down on each one:
  - "Nothing without a reason."
  - "A great programme you can't actually do is a bad programme."
  - "Health and capacity come before performance."
  - "Before you get hurt, you get a message from me."
- Don't memorise it word for word. Learn the point of each section and say it your way.
  If it comes out slightly different, that's better, not worse.
