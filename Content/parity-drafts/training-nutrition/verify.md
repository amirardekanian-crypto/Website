## (c) LESSON FIXES (for the TPS lesson and its English twin `fuel-training`)
1. **"Same day or the next morning"** (After training): the ITF table says rapid refuelling matters when the gap is **under 8 hours**. A morning-after session is usually 12-16 hours away, where the day's total matters. Change to "within about 8 hours" (done in the article).
2. **"One banana or a few dates per hour" is called enough for a long session**: one banana is about 25 g, below the ITF's 30-60 g an hour for 90 min to 3 h. Say it is the low end, and add a few dates if still flat (done in the article). The "about 25 g" is from food tables, Dietitian to check.
3. **Gap under 2 hours = "only a light snack"** can under-fuel two hard sessions in one day (ITF: 1-1.2 g/kg/h for 4 h when under 8 h, as small frequent snacks). The article keeps the light, familiar snacks but adds "a little often" and the ITF line.
4. **Teens' iron and tea**: the lesson says tea "a while before or after meals". Zijp 2000 shows tea matters mostly when meals are low in meat or vitamin C. Keep the advice but not as an absolute (done).
5. **Under-18 coffee and tea**: the lesson attributes the rule to "paediatric experts" in one sentence. The AAP statement is about energy drinks. The coffee/tea ban is Amir's own caution and the article says so ("I'd keep coffee and tea out").
6. **Lesson source note (`_notes`)**: it cites Huang 2023 (PMID 36771479) as "racket sport... table tennis". It is **table tennis**, a different sport. Do not use it for tennis timing. The training-day windows (meal 2-4 h, snack 30-60 min) are practice-based inside the ITF 1-4 h range.
7. **Missing in the lesson, added to the article** (from the ITF full text): daily carbohydrate and protein numbers for adults (3-5 / 5-7 / 6-10 g/kg a day; 1.2-1.8 g/kg protein), the 30-60 minute "dip" caveat, and that the ITF itself calls generic youth targets "difficult". The lesson may keep its qualitative version for juniors.
8. **Vitamin D line**: the ITF statement says skin synthesis can meet needs and the food supply is small (fatty fish, some fortified foods). Eggs are not named there. Sunscreen and training early or late to avoid heat reduce production. The lesson's wording is fine but Dietitian to check.

## (d) Could not verify
- The 2-4 h meal and 30-60 min snack windows as exact training-day windows (inside the ITF 1-4 h range, but no tennis training-day trial).
- Thomas 2016 (PMID 26891166): abstract has no numbers; the numbers are taken from the ITF table adapted from it.
- Whether a banana-per-hour is enough for adolescents. No youth-specific carbohydrate-during-training study was found.
- "Fatty, fried, spicy food sits heavy": only fat and fibre have support.
- Padel: no padel nutrition study cited. Not claimed.
- Ayran as a post-sweat drink and the salt amounts: no study; practice-based.
- Warning-sign list for low energy availability: standard clinical flags, no single source read for the exact list.
