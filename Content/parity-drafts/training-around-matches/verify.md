## (c) LESSON FIXES (the TPS lesson and its English twin)
1. **"4 days in a row of long matches"** must say what the study was: **4-hour matches** on 4 consecutive days in **7 trained men** (Gescheit 2015). Say which numbers were measured when (pre-match jump, post-match sprint) and that serve accuracy, not speed, fell. Soreness is a rise in ratings and CK, not injury.
2. **Taper wording overstates "best".** Bosquet: "seems to be the most efficient", with volume cut 41-60% **exponentially** (not a flat x0.6 then x0.4), in swimmers, cyclists and runners. Mujika & Padilla give a wider range (volume cut up to 60-90%, taper 4 to over 28 days, frequency cut at most 20%) and a ~3% average gain. "A little better" for team sports understates the pooled effects (small to moderate), and repeated-sprint evidence is "limited".
3. **"Any gap is fine" between S&C and tennis has no source.** The one number that exists is Schumann 2022 (3 h or more apart blunts explosive gains less, for aerobic + strength, not tennis). Add it as "if you can", keep "S&C first" labelled as practice plus one 10-player study.
4. **48 h before a match, 48 h after a match and "day before: no main session" are practice-based**, but only the "match day" callout says so. Label them all as practical guidance (the lesson's callout covers only that one section).
5. Lesson says nothing about **sleep or load spikes**, both central to a tournament week and both supported (Schwartz & Simon 2015; Gabbett 2016; Soligard 2016). Add one line each.
6. **Upkeep rule is sound but its evidence is football**, and it should say that a single tournament week off is harmless (Mujika & Padilla 2000), so no panic about a "missed" week. The lesson already has "a tournament week isn't a missed week", so only the source needs adding.
7. The post-match "5-10 min walk and gentle stretches" has no support found: keep it as comfort, not as recovery science.
8. Not touched (course mechanics, out of the article): tournament-period pause, +programme extras counts, A/B/C session rules, 4-/8-/12-/16-week test rules, "repeat last week / go back to week 1" return table, Prime and Upkeep exercise lists.

## (d) Could not verify
- Any study of **how many hours** between a heavy gym session and a match in tennis (or padel). 48 h is a coaching convention.
- Any study of **lifting during a tennis tournament week** (does a short weekly session protect strength or hurt the next match). The maintenance data is football and general adults.
- Any **tennis taper** study. The taper is a borrowed reading.
- That the **3 h gap** (Schumann) applies to tennis. Those studies combine lifting with running or cycling.
- Full text of Bosquet 2007 (sport composition taken from a secondary summary).
- Soreness time course after tennis-style lifting. One study (Soares 2015, PMID 25807025, 16 resistance-trained men, arm exercises only) shows soreness up to 72 h after single-joint work and faster recovery after multi-joint work: not used.
- Benefit of a post-match walk and stretching.
- Padel-specific load or lifting guidance beyond the one mental-fatigue study.
