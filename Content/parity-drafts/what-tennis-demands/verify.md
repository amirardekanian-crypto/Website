## (c) LESSON FIXES (for the TPS lesson `tennis-demands` and its English twin)
1. **Rest is overstated.** "About 3 to 5 times as much rest ... a 6-second point is followed by about 18 to 30 seconds." The review gives work:rest of about 1:1 to 1:4, average rest 10-20 s. Say "up to about 4 times" or give the 1:1-1:4 range.
2. **"Most tennis points last under 10 seconds"** is a distribution claim the sources do not state. They report averages of 5-10 s. Reword to "the average point lasts about 5 to 10 seconds".
3. **"Calf and Achilles: tennis studies don't report them separately"** could not be verified, and tennis injury papers do discuss lower-limb muscle and tendon injury. Reword as "I include them because every step goes through them", or drop the claim about the studies.
4. **"Sole of the foot, especially in women's tennis"**: the source (Gescheit 2017) says "foot", for elite adult women at the Australian Open, second after the shoulder. The lesson's own note also names Robison 2021, which I could not find. Say "foot" and keep the source named as Gescheit.
5. **"The groin works hard in long lunges to the side"** is a mechanism no paper in the set supports. Replace with Dines 2015: groin, hip and abdominal problems can raise shoulder risk.
6. **"The injury data come from studies of tennis players"** hides that most are adult and pro data. Add "mostly adults and pros, a rough guide for juniors".
7. **Serve speed:** "a little faster" is fair, but add that the evidence is low to moderate quality and some programmes did not change serve speed.
8. **Padel is missing** from the lesson. The website audience plays padel. The article adds one short section.
9. Course-only content removed from the article: the 16-week plan, the four blocks and session names, the extra-session rule, the link to the red-flags lesson.
