## (c) LESSON FIXES (the TPS lesson and its English twin `course_en`/hydration-heat should change)
1. **"About 1.5 L per kg lost"** (drinking plan, after training). The ITF 2025 range is 125 to 150% of the loss, so 1.25 to 1.5 L per kg. 1.5 L is the top of the range. Change to "1.25 to 1.5 L" (Farsi «۱٫۲۵ تا ۱٫۵ لیتر») so the lesson and the match-day article agree. The lesson's own `_notes` base 1.5 L on Shirreffs 1996, which shows that more than 100% is needed and that sodium matters, not that 1.5 is the number.
2. **Boys' sweat figure is mislabelled.** The lesson says 14-year-old boys playing "a singles match of about 1 hour 45 minutes in the heat sweated close to 2 litres on average". Bergeron 2007: ~107 min and 1.9 L were a same-day DOUBLES match in 5 boys. Singles lasted ~79 min (8 boys). Fix the wording ("doubles", "5 boys", "same day, after a singles match") or remove the sentence. The lesson's own note already says "doubles about 107 min".
3. **"Players who lost close to 3%"**: the study (Magal 2003) was 11 adult men, about 2.7%, from 2 h of exercise (not a match). Change to "about 2.7%, in 11 men".
4. **"Players who started with less water got hotter as the match went on"** is an association in 8 boys (one match each), not a proven effect. Soften to "in one small study... finished with a higher core temperature".
5. **"Drink to your plan, not only when you're thirsty"**: not tested in tennis, and drink-to-thirst is the position of the exercise-associated hyponatremia consensus (Hew-Butler 2015, abstract not available to me). Say "use your sweat test as a guide, and never force fluid beyond what you lose". The article avoids the line.
6. **Overdrinking evidence is from marathon runners** (Almond 2005), not tennis. Say so.
7. **Cramps and salt**: the lesson reads as if salt loss is the standard cause. The tennis review favours sodium deficit, a systematic review (Schwellnus 2009) favours neuromuscular fatigue and finds the salt/dehydration evidence weak. Say "debated".
8. **Course-only wording** that must not travel with the lesson into any public version: "the card", "the app doesn't save", "Coach tab", "under 16 an adult must attend", lesson links by title, "every session as written" (the article says "don't shorten the session").
9. Consistency note already in the lesson `_notes` stays open: learn-4 warmup-ramp heat warn says "drink water" for every heat sign including confusion, hydration-heat says sips only if fully alert and not vomiting. And «آن روزها آرام‌تر شروع کنید» (learn-4) clashes with "session stays as written". Amir's call.

- Whole heat stroke list: emergency call first, whole-body cold or ice water up to the neck with the head held, fallback dousing plus fanning, "give nothing by mouth if confused", "stay with them until help arrives".
- Emergency numbers (115, 999, 112, 911).
