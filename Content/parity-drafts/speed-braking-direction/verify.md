## (c) LESSON FIXES (TPS lesson and its English twin)
1. **Ladder sentence.** "Reaction training beat ladder training" is too broad. Say what it was: a computer-based reaction program against a rope ladder, 64 male college basketball players, 4 weeks, one study (Zhang 2026). Same fix in the Farsi.
2. **"Braking needs leg strength".** Too narrow. Strength (eccentric above all) is linked to braking in small studies, but Harper 2022 also names technique, reactive and isometric strength. Add "and technique".
3. **"In studies on 180 degree turns, one of the steps before the last ..."** Be exact: one lab study, 20 male soccer players, 505 test, biggest braking force on the third-last step (Dos'Santos 2021). The "up to 2.7 times" line should say "peak force in the first 50 ms".
4. **Distances.** "Court-length distances" in the late blocks, against Kovacs 2006: no sprint longer than the longest run per shot, stop-start sprints up to 20 m. A tennis singles court is 23.77 m long. Amir should check that the court-pattern distances stay at or under about 20 m per run, or label them practice-based.
5. **Label more things as practice.** The lesson labels the brake cap as practical but not the 48 hour rule, the one-session-a-day rule, the 5 to 10 m starts, "reps don't rise as turns harden", or "fast approaches one week later for under-16s". None has a study behind it.
6. **Padel.** The lesson and its twin speak only about tennis. This article adds one padel paragraph (about 11 m per point, Ramon-Llin 2021). Optional for the lesson.
7. Programme names (Court B, "+ Speed and Agility", Blocks 1 to 4, Build-Up Run, "the card") and the lesson links were removed. Exercise names kept: Crossover Start Sprint, Turn and Go, Wide-Ball Brake and Recover.
