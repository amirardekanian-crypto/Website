Six ways to make
the cards feel alive

All six are on the Night Court card you picked, with the clay edge rule removed. The split that matters is not how much moves but why it moves.

Event — motion that means something

Fires only when something actually happened: a record was beaten, a number changed since you last looked. Rare, so it still lands after six months.

Ambient — motion that decorates

Always running. Reads as premium for about a week, then as noise on a screen athletes open every day. Use sparingly, and never more than one at a time.

What I'd actually ship

5 (New best) and 1 (Status light), and nothing else. Between them you get a real celebration when a record falls, and a quiet dot that tells you the day is not finished yet — and the dot stops when you finish it, which is the whole point. Add 2 (line draws itself) if you want the screen to feel like it boots. Everything beyond that is motion competing with motion.

Two things that come free either way. All of this respects prefers-reduced-motion — an athlete with motion sensitivity set on their phone sees the cards perfectly still, which is already wired into this file. And every animation here is CSS transform and opacity only, so none of it costs battery or causes the card to re-layout.

Still need from you: whether the habit card carries a live level and streak at all. Ideas 1 and 4 are both built on it — without live habit data that card has nothing to animate, and only the two Ceiling ideas survive.
