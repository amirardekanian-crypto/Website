# The kit: every piece, how to call it, what its knobs do

> Generated by `tools/kit_docs.js` from the kit itself. Do not edit by hand: change the piece's `doc` and `params`, then run the tool.

Call a piece with `KIT.draw(id, ctx, t, params)`; a scene change with `KIT.transition(id, params)` or `KIT.cut(id, ctx, A, B, p, params)`; a look with `KIT.use(id)`.
A param you leave out takes the default shown. `at` is always a time in seconds; positions are canvas pixels. See `README.md` for the conventions.

## Data: looks, type pairs, feel, house rules, finish settings

**look**

- `ink-coral` (#74 Ink and Coral): Black stage, bone type, one coral dot.
- `coral-block` (#75 Coral Block): A full coral screen with black type.
- `primary-paper` (#76 Primary Paper): Warm paper with ink, coral and cobalt shapes.
- `cobalt-deep` (#77 Cobalt Deep): Deep blue with pearl pink, lilac and mint highlights.
- `night-spectrum` (#78 Night Spectrum): Black night with a six-colour spectrum of light.
- `daylight-ui` (#79 Daylight UI): Bright screen, white cards, cobalt and one coral switch.
- `colour-plates` (#80 Colour Plates): Flat plates in ink, cobalt, mint, coral, bone and lilac.
- `reel`: The showreel look: ink and bone with a hot coral, Unbounded and Instrument Serif. Claude's own, not Amir's brand.
- `brand`: Amir's brand on a dark stage: clay is the one accent, green is the fix, bone is normal, grey is detail. No yellow.
- `signal-colours` (#175 Signal Colours): Five colours, five jobs. Bone is normal, clay is the key word, green is the fix, grey is detail, a clay line is a mistake.
- `brand`: Amir's brand on a dark stage: clay is the one accent, green is the fix, bone is normal, grey is detail. No yellow.

**font**

- `heavy-italic` (#81 Heavy and Italic): A very wide black sans with an elegant italic serif.
- `mono-tag` (#82 Mono Tag): Small tracked capitals in a coding font, for labels and timecode.
- `ui-sans` (#83 UI Sans): A tight, clean sans for interface text and numbers.

**ease**

- `settle` (#84 Settle): Fast arrival, long soft landing.
- `overshoot` (#85 Overshoot): Goes past the mark, then settles back.
- `glide` (#86 Glide): Slow, fast, slow.
- `rubber` (#87 Rubber): Wobbles before it comes to rest.
- `bounce` (#88 Bounce): Drops and bounces to rest.
- `windup` (#89 Windup): Pulls back first, then goes.
- `spring` (#90 Spring): Real spring physics: stiffness and damping.
- `stagger` (#91 Stagger): Start each piece a little after the last.

**rule**

- `beat-grid` (#92 Beat Grid): 128 beats a minute. Eight bars make exactly 15 seconds.
- `downbeat` (#93 Downbeat): Every scene change lands on the first beat of a bar.
- `anticipation` (#94 Anticipation): Land one or two frames early so it feels exactly on the beat.
- `cue-sheet` (#95 Cue Sheet): The picture writes down every hit it makes. The sound is built from that list, then delayed 12 ms so it never leads.

**finish**

- `shutter` (#64 Shutter): How long the camera shutter stays open on each frame. Longer means more motion blur.
- `jolt` (#65 Jolt): How hard a big hit shakes and punches the camera.
- `halo` (#66 Halo): A soft glow around the brightest parts of the picture.
- `prism` (#67 Prism): The colour fringe at the edges, like a cheap lens. Grows on every hit.
- `film` (#68 Film): Fine film grain over everything.
- `vignette` (#69 Vignette): The corners fall off into dark.
