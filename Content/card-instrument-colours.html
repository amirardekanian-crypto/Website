Two notes

Dark mode. Four of these are dark surfaces on a light page. In dark mode the app's own cards are #202020, so Ink and Warm Ink would nearly vanish into the background — they need a lighter surface or a visible border when body[data-dark] is on. The three green and clay ones stay legible in both without changing anything, which is a real point in their favour.

The habit number is still a decision. All five show a live level and streak. This app deliberately holds no habit state today — reading AA Proof's own keys is cheap and I already do it for body weight, but say the word either way and I will build the card to match.
