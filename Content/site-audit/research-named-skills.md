Skip both.
Borrow: measured evidence; script-generic RTL checks (letter-spacing on cursive script, line-height, bidi hazards such as `$180` inside a Farsi line, physical CSS in RTL, dir/lang, truncation); the parity pass (EN vs FA: renamed features, missing sections, mismatched numbers); a form field dump (labels, autocomplete, aria-invalid, disabled submits); 24 px tap targets; focus-ring contrast; head/SEO tags.
Misfires on Farsi: its engine is Arabic-specific. Plural, dialect, gendered-imperative and hamza checks give false positives; the digit check covers Western and Arabic-Indic digits only, not Persian (U+06F0-06F9), so mixed Persian/Western digits pass silently; the untranslated check runs only for lang="ar"; parity may not read Persian digits (UNVERIFIED). New and lightly reviewed: read the probes once before the first run.
