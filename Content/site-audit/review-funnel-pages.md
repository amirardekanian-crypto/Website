### What works well (keep)
- **links:** 8 KB, 0.45 s load (1.5 s on slow 4G). One clay primary button, 87-113 px tap targets, clear colour logic (white = product, dark = demo, clay = main), plain badges, clean Farsi (no Arabic letters, ZWNJ right, Persian digits).
- **proof:** honest and specific ("not instant", "no card", the locked Workout said out loud). Short labelled form, right keyboards, consent line and privacy link right under the button, soft coaching CTA at the end.
- **partner-fa:** plain, fair terms (10% of what is paid, first payment and every renewal, no cap, 7 working days, "not employment"); the "personal recommendation first" ladder is useful.
- All three: no console errors, no sideways scroll at 360 / 390 / 820 / 1440. `noindex` on links and partner-fa is deliberate and right.
