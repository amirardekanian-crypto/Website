### Findings (each ends: effort, risk, needs Amir Y/N)
1. **form-en-01** P1, Length vs promise, M-L, med, Y. Home promises 2 minutes; the form says ~5 and asks 27 to 30 questions, 18 required, 7.8 phone screens. My estimate: 2.5 min required-only, 4 to 6 typical. Sleep, stress, nutrition, equipment and lifts are only needed after the call; the "season" the hero promises is never asked. *Evidence:* counter 0/27, 1/29, 2/30 (real viewport); form.html:551, index.html:1668. *Fix:* now, home note "About 5 minutes". Then A) 9-field Apply (plan, name, email, WhatsApp, sport and level, goal, days, pain yes/no, next event) plus a prep form in your reply: true 2 min, less health data from strangers, second form to build. B) Same questions in 5 saved steps: feels shorter, needs a rewrite. C) One page cut to ~18, rest under "Optional: speeds up our call": cheapest. I'd do C, then A if 02 shows drop-off.
2. **form-en-02** P1, Abandonment, S-M, low, Y. Nothing is saved until the last tap: a reload took 5/29 answers back to 0/27, and you never learn who quit at question 12. The only analytics event is "Form Submitted". *Evidence:* verified; storage empty; form.html:1407. *Fix:* draft in localStorage (S); Plausible events for started, section reached, field that errored (S); later save name and WhatsApp after section 1, with a notice (M).
3. **form-en-03** P1, iPhone zoom, S, low, N. Inputs, selects and textareas are 14 px (FA 15 px); iOS Safari and in-app browsers zoom on focus below 16 px, so the form jumps at every field. *Evidence:* computed 14/15 px; form.html:236, form-fa.html:71; no iPhone test. *Fix:* font-size:16px on input, select, textarea.
4. **form-fa-01** P1, FA contact field, S, low, N. Contact, email and counter lack dir="ltr": typed "+98 912 345 6789" shows "6789 345 912 98+", "@sara_tennis" shows "sara_tennis@". The placeholder shows Persian digits, and coach.html waLink() keeps only 0-9, so such a number gets no WhatsApp link. Counter "۰ / ۲۷" reads "۲۷ / ۰" (your LTR-counter rule). *Evidence:* real viewport; form-fa.html:197, 233-234; coach.html:4719. *Fix:* dir="ltr" on #contact, #em, #pCount; Latin placeholder; convert Persian digits to 0-9 and "09…" to "+989…" on send.
5. **form-en-04** P1, Success and submit, M, low, Y. The success screen is a dead end: no link or button, "reach out directly" with no way to, no word on how you reply, focus stays behind it. Submit waits for both calls with no timeout (unverified from Iran). *Evidence:* 0 links in overlay (toggled, not submitted); form.html:1056, 1397; form-fa.html:632. *Fix:* "Message Amir on WhatsApp" (prefilled with name and plan) and "Back to site"; say how and when you reply; focus the heading; succeed when either call lands, 10 s timeout; inline error, not alert.
6. **form-fa-02** P1, FA keyboard, S, low, N. Pills and radio cards show nothing on keyboard focus (0 :focus-visible rules); FA also lacks a main landmark, skip link and dialog name, and emoji are read aloud. *Evidence:* real viewport; form-fa.html:78-94. *Fix:* copy EN's :focus-visible rules (form.html:287, 331); add main, skip link, aria-labelledby, aria-hidden on .ic.
7. **form-en-05** P2, Programme step, S-M, low, Y. First question is a required price pick on bare cards (no "what's included", no "Not sure" though home says you'll help choose), above a coupon box. Home pricing buttons go to #apply, so the plan is picked twice; ?programme= and ?code= are ignored. *Evidence:* form.html:588-612; index.html:1229, 1243, 1261. *Fix:* add "Not sure yet" and an "all plans include…" line; read ?programme= and ?code= (affiliate links); hide the code behind "Have a code?"; show the discounted price.
8. **form-en-06** P2, Sticky bars, S, low, N. Nav plus progress bar cover 113 px of an 844 px phone (FA 111), about a quarter of what is left with the keyboard up; with no scroll-padding, Shift+Tab hides First Name fully under them. The bar counts 27 to 30 questions though 18 are required. *Evidence:* real viewport; form.html:93-135 (0 / 0 in HTML, 0 / 27 live). *Fix:* html{scroll-padding-top:130px}; slim bar and no site nav on phones; count required ("5 of 18").
9. **form-en-07** P2, Sliders, S/M, low, Y. Pressing Tab on a slider records the default (sleep 7, stress 5) as an answer, and nothing tells screen readers it is unanswered (no aria-valuetext). Tracks are 133 and 178 px wide (13 to 18 px a step); the grey thumb looks disabled. *Evidence:* Tab only set 7 and the counter +1; form.html:1241-1244; form-fa.html:485-487. *Fix:* commit only on input and value keys; aria-valuetext "not answered". Better: 1-5 tap chips (the app's check is 1-5).
10. **form-en-08** P2, Validation, M, med, N. Errors show only on submit, one at a time, always "Please answer this question" (also for Age outside 10-80); required questions are unmarked and 6 optional ones look required. *Evidence:* form.html:1284-1295, 1367-1379. *Fix:* mark "(optional)"; validate on blur; specific messages; "3 questions left" by the button.
11. **form-en-09** P2, Field types, M, med, N. Contact mixes handle and phone, no keyboard hint; Age is type=number so Persian digits on FA are dropped (verified: ۲۸ leaves it empty); FA has no autocomplete (WCAG 1.3.5); 7 short lists are selects. *Evidence:* form-fa.html:229-239; Chromium test. *Fix:* WhatsApp number (type=tel) plus optional Instagram; Age as text, inputmode=numeric, digits converted; autocomplete tokens; chips for 5 options or fewer.
12. **form-en-10** P2, Trust and consent, S-M, low, Y. Privacy notice 2.1 rests injury data on explicit consent, but the form has only a 12 px grey "By submitting, you agree" line; nothing by the injury questions says who sees them, nothing says "no commitment until we speak" (home does); FA links an English-only policy. *Evidence:* form.html:1045-1048; privacy.html 2.1; form-fa.html:430. *Fix:* by the button, "No commitment until we speak. I reply within 48 hours." plus a tick "Only Amir sees my injury answers" (not legal advice); Farsi policy.
13. **form-en-11** P2, Palette, S, low, N. "Set" and "University" show a yellow ball, "Semi-Pro" orange; emoji add no meaning and differ per device (FA flag shows "IR" on Windows). *Evidence:* tile-00, real viewport; form.html:596, 715, 719. *Fix:* plain text cards, or small green and clay SVG marks.
14. **form-en-12** P2, Layout and structure, S, low, N. In one column the last field of a pair has no bottom margin, so labels touch the input above (0 px against 22-36); FA selects show two chevrons; 10 radio or checkbox questions have no group name; the EN sleep row overflows a 320 px phone. *Evidence:* measured; FA tile-04; form.html:200; form-fa.html:62, 69-74. *Fix:* drop the :last-child margin rule in .row-2/.row-3; appearance:none on FA select; fieldset and legend; min-width:0 on the range.
15. **form-fa-03** P2, EN and FA parity, S, low, Y.
