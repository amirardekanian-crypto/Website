- UTS form: six steps, 52 px fields, inline errors, hidden questions clear their answers, one unticked consent per purpose, an honest under-18 path. UTS red/blue is right. The Tube line draws correctly on phone (390, 360), tablet and desktop.
- Privacy names Supabase, Web3Forms, Plausible, GitHub, Google Fonts and YouTube, with a basis per purpose, rights and the ICO. Checked true: jump.html only requests fonts, YouTube loads only on tap, intake rows are coach-read-only.
- Terms: the health disclaimer is a red callout near the top with a stop-if list; Farsi is a faithful translation.
- Etminan: form hidden behind a tap, separate parent ticks, a "cost goes through the academy" line. Alireza (EN/DV) versus Faraz (FA) is deliberate (commit 8947a59). Neon green, lime and amber are a deliberate co-brand (CSS comment L34-36), not drift.

### Cross-page patterns you noticed
- Google Fonts loads on 17 public root pages (the IP goes to Google; privacy §2.8 admits it). Self-hosting also helps Iran and removes font-swap shifts. `/assets/fonts/Vazirmatn-Variable.woff2` already exists.
- Legal text lags the product: forms say "by submitting you agree", the notice claims explicit consent, Farsi pages link to an English notice, terms date from April.
- Shared tokens fail contrast: `--text-muted` #8A8A8A is 3.23:1 and `--text-dim` #B0A99E is 2.18:1 on paper (12 to 13 px text). Small clay text is 4.12:1; #BC4E2A passes (4.61:1). Shared footer links are 19 px tall.
