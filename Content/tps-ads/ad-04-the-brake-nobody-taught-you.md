# AD 04 · The brake nobody taught you

> Status: **brief** · 2026-09-21 · Angle A2 + A4 · Visual V1 + V4 · Ask C1
> ⚠️ Refusal 3 binds hardest here. This ad **teaches**; it never warns.

---

## PART A · THE BRIEF

**1 · CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVE**
Sells the **$17 course**, counted in **WhatsApp messages**. Second job: saves. A player with a sore
knee sends this to the friend with the same sore knee.

**2 · TARGET AUDIENCE**
Players carrying knee pain they have decided to live with. **What they already believe:** that the
pain is wear and tear, or bad luck, or something to stretch — and that nothing about it is trainable.

**3 · THE PROBLEM, IN THEIR WORDS**
> "Some kind of injury — knee pain from deceleration."

P3, and the desire D2 (keep playing for years). ⚠️ **They said deceleration**, not "my knee hurts" —
that word is what makes this ad possible, because braking is a skill and skills are trainable.

**4 · THE OBJECTION IT KILLS**
**"I don't trust an online course."** Sideways: by being demonstrably more useful than the thing they
paid for. A coach who explains where the load actually goes, and who tells them to see a doctor when
it matters, is a coach worth trusting.

**5 · CORE MESSAGE**
Braking is a skill nobody taught you, and untrained braking has to be absorbed somewhere.

**6 · CTA**
**C1 · the demo.** «هفته‌ی ۱ رو رایگان امتحان کن».
⚠️ The doctor referral comes **before** the ask, never after — otherwise it reads as a disclaimer
bolted onto a sale.

---

## PART B · THE IDEA

**7 · CREATIVE IDEA**
**A2 Wrong Tool** carried on **A4's** safety rules. Its own thing: one picture does the entire
explanation — a slide mark ending in a ridge of piled clay. The viewer sees the force before he names
it.

Three hooks:
1. «زانوت موقع ترمز درد می‌گیره؟ ترمز یه مهارته که کسی یادت نداده.»
2. «تو تنیس، آسیب معمولاً موقع ایستادن اتفاق می‌افته، نه دویدن.»
3. «این ردّ پا رو ببین. تمامِ نیرو همین‌جاست.»

**8 · EMOTIONAL DIRECTION**
**Understanding.** Not alarm, not reassurance — the specific relief of finally knowing *why*. It is
«هیچی بی‌دلیل نیست» applied to something that hurts.
⚠️ **No fear.** No "if you don't fix this now". No ticking-clock language. The course's own position
is that fast growth and normal soreness are not danger signs; this ad agrees with it.
