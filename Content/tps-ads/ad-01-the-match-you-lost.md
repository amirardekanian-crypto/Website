## PART A · THE BRIEF

**1 · CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVE**
Sells the **$17 tennis course**, counted in **WhatsApp messages**. Organic Instagram, Farsi.
Why now: it is the widest-reaching of the five — every competitive player has lost this match, so it
works on someone who has never heard of Amir.

**2 · TARGET AUDIENCE**
Iranian tennis and padel players who train their tennis and not their body. **What they already
believe:** that the gap between them and the player who beat them is technical, and that more
practice is the answer.

**3 · THE PROBLEM, IN THEIR WORDS**
> "In the third set my energy is low and my legs are tired."
> "I'm not quick to the balls — I want to be faster."

P1 + P2 + P5, and the desire D1 (win the matches they are currently losing).

**4 · THE OBJECTION IT KILLS**
**"It's free on YouTube."** Not by arguing — by showing that the thing they lost was never a
technique problem, so no amount of technique content was ever going to fix it. It reframes the
category before it sells anything.

**5 · CORE MESSAGE**
You did not lose that match with your racket. You lost it on the second step.

**6 · CTA**
**C1 · the demo.** «هفته‌ی ۱ رو رایگان امتحان کن» — verbatim off `links.html`.
No WhatsApp reply needed; this ad asks for a free look, not money.

## PART B · THE IDEA

**7 · CREATIVE IDEA**
Angle **A8 · The match you lost**. Its own thing: it never mentions fitness until after the viewer
has already relived the match. The first fifteen seconds are a memory, not an argument.

Three hooks — read out loud, pick one:
1. «تکنیکش از تو بدتر بود. پاهاش نه.»
2. «یه بازی رو یادت بیار که باختی به کسی که ازت بدتر بازی می‌کرد.»
3. «تو ست سوم پات نمی‌کشه؟ مشکل از ریه‌ت نیست.»

**8 · EMOTIONAL DIRECTION**
**Recognition, then relief.** He describes their match more accurately than they could, which earns
the authority; then the relief is that it was not a talent problem. ⚠️ No shame — the viewer is
never the fool in this story.
