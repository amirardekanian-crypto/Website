# takes/ — Amir's own recordings

**Put your filmed take here.** One file per ad, named for the ad:
`ad-03-slow-and-bulky.mp4`, or `<slug>.mp4` for a new one.

This is the folder Claude can actually reach. Higgsfield's download servers are blocked from the
web sessions, so anything Claude has to cut must arrive through the repo.

**Alongside the video, drop a `.md` with the same name** holding the script you actually said —
Claude times the edit against your real delivery rather than a planned one. If the script is only in
your head, say so and Claude will time it off the audio instead.

## How to film

Vertical phone, lens at eye height, chest-up. Soft indirect light in front of you — a window to the
side, an overcast day, a gym doorway. **Shoot dull, never bright:** the house grade deepens shadows
and warms highlights, and it can rescue a dull frame but never a blown-out one.

Plain dark clothes, no logos, **nothing yellow** anywhere in shot. Behind you: a plain wall, a gym,
a court fence — nothing with text on it.

**One take of the whole script**, not shot by shot. Leave two seconds of silence at the top and the
tail. Two or three whole takes beats twelve fragments: the cutaways go over your pauses, which is
what hides a stumble.

⚠️ **Never film exercise form.** A grip, a push-off, laying a tape measure, marking a heel — fine.
A rep is not.
