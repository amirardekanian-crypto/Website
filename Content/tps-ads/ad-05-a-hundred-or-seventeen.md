# AD 05 · A hundred dollars or seventeen

> Status: **brief** · 2026-09-21 · Angle A5 + the price anchor · Visual V5 · Ask C2
> ⚠️ The only ad in the set that asks for money. Runs to people who have already seen the demo.

---

## PART A · THE BRIEF

**1 · CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVE**
Sells the **$17 course** and asks for the sale. Counted in **WhatsApp messages** — this is the ad
that number exists to measure. Not a reach ad; it is aimed at the people the other four warmed up.

**2 · TARGET AUDIENCE**
People who have seen the demo, or read the page, and not bought. **What they already believe:** that
$17 is expensive — because they are comparing it against nothing. Amir considers $17 very cheap; that
gap is the entire ad.

**3 · THE PROBLEM, IN THEIR WORDS**
> They just ask the price.

Not a training problem — a **unit** problem. They have no reference for what a 16-week programme
costs, so the number floats free and lands as "expensive".

**4 · THE OBJECTION IT KILLS**
**"I want a real coach watching me."** Answered honestly: that is the personal programme, it exists,
and it costs about $100 for the same 16 weeks. This course is the same method, self-guided, at a
sixth of the price. Naming the more expensive thing is what makes the cheap thing legible.

**5 · CORE MESSAGE**
The same sixteen weeks, written by the same coach, for a sixth of the price — because you run it
yourself.

**6 · CTA**
**C2 · WhatsApp.** «خرید از واتساپ» — verbatim off the price card on `/tennis/`.
**Ships with `WHATSAPP.md` T3**, and if they answer «گرونه», **T3b**. The ad's closing line and T3's
first line carry the same promise: $17, once, no expiry.

---

## PART B · THE IDEA

**7 · CREATIVE IDEA**
**A5 Order** with the price anchor doing the work. Its own thing: it is the **quietest** ad of the
five. No cuts to chase, no argument to win — one dark plate, real screens, and a comparison stated
once. It has to feel expensive to be believed as cheap.

Three hooks:
1. «۱۰۰ دلار یا ۱۷ دلار؟ همون ۱۶ هفته.»
2. «برنامه‌ی اختصاصیِ من ماهی ۲۵ دلاره. این یکی کلاً ۱۷ دلاره.»
3. «یه‌بار می‌خری. تاریخِ انقضا نداره.»

**8 · EMOTIONAL DIRECTION**
**Calm certainty.** No urgency, no scarcity, no countdown — nothing is running out, which is the
point of a product with no expiry. The feeling to leave behind is *that is obviously fair*.
