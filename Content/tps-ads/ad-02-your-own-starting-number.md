## PART A · THE BRIEF

**1 · CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVE**
Sells the **$17 course**, counted in **WhatsApp messages** — but its first job is **reach**. This is
the one built to travel: it gives a whole test away, so it earns saves and sends, which is the only
distribution an organic ad has.

**2 · TARGET AUDIENCE**
Players who train seriously and have never measured anything. **What they already believe:** that
progress is something you feel, and that if they are not improving they are not working hard enough.

**3 · THE PROBLEM, IN THEIR WORDS**
> "However much I train, I don't get better."

P4. Underneath it: they have no way to know whether the last six months did anything.

**4 · THE OBJECTION IT KILLS**
**"It's free on YouTube."** Free content hands out exercises. It cannot give anyone a baseline, a
protocol and a retest four weeks later — and without those, nobody can tell whether any of it worked.
This is the sharpest answer in the whole bank, and it is Amir's own top annoyance.

**5 · CORE MESSAGE**
If you cannot name one number that changed, you have been repeating, not progressing.

**6 · CTA**
**C3 · a comment.** «عددت رو برام بنویس.» Costs the viewer nothing, the thread is itself reach, and
every commenter is someone who just did a test Amir designed. The demo link stays in the bio.

## PART B · THE IDEA

**7 · CREATIVE IDEA**
**A1 Measurement** carried by **A7 The Plateau**. Its own thing: the ad is an *instruction*, not a
pitch — the viewer can complete it on their living-room floor before the ad ends.

Three hooks:
1. «شش ماهه تمرین می‌کنی. کدوم عددت عوض شده؟»
2. «یه سال تمرین کردی و همون‌جایی. داری تکرار می‌کنی، نه پیشرفت.»
3. «بلند شو. این تست رو همین الان بده.»

**8 · EMOTIONAL DIRECTION**
**Curiosity, then resolve.** The question is uncomfortable for two seconds and then becomes something
they can act on immediately. ⚠️ The discomfort must never tip into shame — the plateau is framed as a
missing tool, never as laziness.
