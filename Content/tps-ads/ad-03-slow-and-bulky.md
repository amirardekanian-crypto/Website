# AD 03 · Slow and bulky

> Status: **brief** · 2026-09-21 · Angle A12 · Visual V1 + V4 · Ask C1
> ⚠️ **Film this one first.** It is the only ad that answers the objection stopping people *before*
> they consider buying anything, and it costs nothing to make.

---

## PART A · THE BRIEF

**1 · CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVE**
Sells the **$17 course**, counted in **WhatsApp messages** — but its real job is to unblock every
other ad. Somebody who believes the gym will ruin their movement cannot be sold a gym programme by
any angle.

**2 · TARGET AUDIENCE**
Players who have deliberately avoided strength training. **What they already believe:** that lifting
makes you big, and big makes you slow — and they have watched it happen to someone.

**3 · THE PROBLEM, IN THEIR WORDS**
> "Going to the gym will make me slower or bulky."

P9 — reported by Amir 2026-09-21. Not a complaint about their body, a belief about training.

**4 · THE OBJECTION IT KILLS**
**"The gym will make me slow and bulky."** Head on, and the only way that works: **agree with it
first.** They are right about the training they have seen. That training is bodybuilding, which is
also Amir's own complaint about how tennis players get coached — their fear and his argument are
the same fact from two sides.

**5 · CORE MESSAGE**
You are right to be afraid of that kind of training. This is not that kind of training.

**6 · CTA**
**C1 · the demo.** «هفته‌ی ۱ رو رایگان امتحان کن» — verbatim off `links.html`.

## PART B · THE IDEA

**7 · CREATIVE IDEA**
Angle **A12 · The bulk myth**. Its own thing: **the ad concedes in the first five seconds.** Nobody
expects a coach to say "you're right" about the thing he sells, and that turn is what buys the next
thirty seconds. The argument is then carried by one hard cut — the gym they picture, then the gym
this is.

Three hooks:
1. «می‌ترسی باشگاه کندت کنه؟ حق داری — ولی نه از این تمرین.»
2. «باشگاه حجیمت می‌کنه؟ اون باشگاهی که دیدی، آره.»
3. «تنیسورها از وزنه می‌ترسن. دلیلشون هم بد نیست.»

**8 · EMOTIONAL DIRECTION**
**Being understood, then irritation at the method.** First he takes their side, then he moves the
anger onto bodybuilding-for-athletes. ⚠️ The irritation is aimed at a **method**, never at a coach,
a gym or a person (refusal 1) — and the coaches watching must be able to share this.
