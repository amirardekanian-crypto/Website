Ad plan · Tennis Performance System · 21 Sep 2026 · draft 2

Five ads, five different jobs

Five ads that do not overlap. Read top to bottom they are a funnel: a stranger who has never thought about their body, then the lifter who cannot see it on court, then a parent, then a free test worth saving, then the close. Every line of copy comes from the course itself — the lessons, the test protocols, the block names, the price page. Nothing is invented. Each Farsi line below is marked with where it came from, so you can see at a glance which 9 of the 30 lines are mine to be argued with.

A new audience

New shoes again? باز کفش نو لازم داریم؟

4 credits
Who it talks to
Parents of 13–17 year olds. They pay for the tennis, and nobody is selling them this.
The idea
The course already has a teen version (13–15), a growth-spurt lesson and a parents' guide. The hook is the course's own first sign of a growth spurt — a sentence every parent of a teenager has said out loud.
How it looks
Home. Warm, still, domestic. No court, no gym, no iron. The only one of the five that does not look like a fitness ad — which is exactly why a parent stops on it.

Value first

Your own starting number عدد شروع خودت

4 credits
Who it talks to
Anyone who trains and has never measured anything.
The idea
Give away a whole test. The broad jump is already free in the demo, so the ad's payoff is a real page in the app, not a teaser. And the course's honest position is unusual enough to be the ad: it never tells you whether your number is good — you only ever race yourself.
How it looks
Measurement. A tape measure runs down the screen and the type hangs off it like ticks. The most graphic of the five.

The close

Once, forever یک بار، برای همیشه

reuses what we own · 0 credits
Who it talks to
Anyone who has already seen the demo and not bought. This is the only one of the five that asks for the sale.
The idea
Three objections die in one ad: the price, the subscription fear, and the internet. In Iran «after the first sign-in it works offline» is a felt benefit, not a feature bullet.
How it looks
No cuts at all. One dark plate, floodlights, drifting haze, and numbers landing on it. The quietest of the five on purpose — this one has to feel expensive.
