Where this stands

Two things you settled on 21 September, and what is still open.

  • ✓Rebuilt off real problems. Every ad now opens on something a customer actually said. The parents' ad is gone — parents are not in your audience — and the gym-vs-court ad is replaced by the bulk myth, which is a thing people really believe.
  • ✓Ad 03 is new, and may be the most valuable. «باشگاه منو کند و حجیم می‌کنه» is the only objection that stops someone before they consider buying anything at all. It is also your own argument from the other side: they fear the gym because the gym they have seen is bodybuilding.
  • ✓Iran prices only. $25/month, so about $100 for the same 16 weeks, against $17. The $200 and the £50 UK session are not prices an Iranian player can buy and never appear in a Farsi ad.
  • ✓You are in four of the five. Talking head plus b-roll is the default now. Film the whole script in one take — the cutaways hide any stumble.
  • ?The {{DRAFTS}} draft lines, and the hooks above all. Those are the first two seconds and you have to say them out loud.
  • ?Your story. Still the missing piece — it blocks the one angle that answers "I don't trust an online course from anyone", and now that you are on camera it is the ad only you can make.
