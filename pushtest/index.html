Does a notification reach this phone?

One question, two minutes. This page sends nothing anywhere. Whatever it finds out stays on your screen until you copy it and send it to Amir yourself.

On an iPhone, add this to your Home Screen first

Apple only lets a web page send notifications once it is installed. In Safari it cannot work at all, so do this first:

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Close Safari and open Push test from your Home Screen.
  4. Come back to this page there and carry on.

Read this before you tap

A phone gives permission to a whole website, once. amirardekani.com has one permission, shared by the training app, AA Proof and this page.

So if you tap Don't allow, this phone can never be sent a notification by any of them again, until you go into your browser settings and undo it. There is no second chance. Tap Allow.
