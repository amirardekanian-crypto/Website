# 10. Find, the phone's bar, one-tap outcomes (2026-11-23)

Three picks, all in `coach.html`. Amir now uses the iPhone (the doorbell sends him to athletes and Leads); Exercises is a PC job.

- **Find.** A ⌕ Find button before Refresh in the top bar (its word hides below 720 px and from 900 to 1339 px), `/` when no text box has the focus, and Ctrl/Cmd+K anywhere open one panel under the bar (`qfSet()`, `qfMatches()`). It matches athletes with the roster search's own text (`rosterHay()`, at most 8: avatar, name, tier and cycle or "no programme") and applications by name, email or contact (at most 5: "Application · FA|EN · arrived …"). Nothing typed shows the five athletes with the latest session. Up and Down move the highlight, Enter opens it (the first by default), Esc closes and returns the focus to the button. An athlete opens `#a/<id>`; an application is made the one on show (`openApplication()`) and opens `#leads`. It never stays open with ⚙ Tools or the phone's More sheet, and any screen change, or a click outside it, closes it.
- **The phone's bar (below 600 px)** is Today · Athletes · Leads · More. The notes-to-read badge is on Athletes, the new-applications badge on Leads. Exercises moved into More (note "The Spine, qualities, coverage"), and More's own badge is the Mailbag count (nothing when none). `BAR_IDS` in `paintBotBar()`. Below 720 px the top bar also drops the brand's second half ("· THE BOX") so four buttons fit at 600 px.
- **One-tap outcomes.** In an application's closing row, "What happened" is buttons: **Became a client**, **No reply**, **Not a fit**, and a quiet **Other…**. No reply and Not a fit save at once; Became a client unfolds Which athlete (pre-selected by `intakeGuess()`) and a Save, because a client needs an athlete; Other… unfolds the old select of all outcomes with its Save. All four roads end in `intakeSetOutcome(id, outcome, athlete, btn)` (the same `set_intake_outcome` call, the same words, a saved outcome moves a new application to Handled). The saved outcome's button is shown pressed (green). No outcome button is primary; the Save in an unfolded part is. Mark handled, Archive and Reopen stay quiet.

**Tests.** `find-anything`, `intake-one-tap`; `phone-bottom-bar`, `intake-outcome` and `intake-list-detail` follow the new controls.
