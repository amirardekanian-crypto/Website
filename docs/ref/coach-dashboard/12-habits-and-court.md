# 12. Habits and court time, in the file and across everyone (2026-11-23)

Amir picked direction A, "The Grid", to see each athlete's habits and court time in their file and across everyone. All in `coach.html`; nothing new is written to the server.

- **The engine is the athlete app's.** `assets/js/habits-core.js` (generated, never edited) says what a habit is and when it is met; `habBind(H, a)` binds it to ONE athlete (their `<id>_hab_cfg` merged over `defaultCfg()` as `program.html` `habLoad()` does, their `<id>_hab_log`, then `anchorRoster()`), asks, and the next athlete re-binds. A habit that did not exist on a day (`rosterOn(day)`) draws no box, and does not count against the 7-day summary. If the script does not arrive, no grid is drawn and the banner says *the habit engine*. It is `defer`red, so a draw that found it missing is done again from its `onload`.
- **Their file.** On The work, under Five weeks: **Habits and court · last 14 days** (`habGridPanelC()`, `#habGrid`). A Court row first (the day's minutes, dark when any entry was hard, the title lists each entry), then a row per habit the athlete had in those 14 days in catalogue order, customs last. The last column is the last 7 days: `avg · met/days-it-existed` for counts, `met of days` for check habits, `4 h 30 this week` for Court; it is clay when the habit was met on fewer than three of the days it existed (fewer than all of them when it existed fewer than three). No panel when there is no habit log day and no court entry in the 14 days.
- **Everyone.** Athletes has a **Training | Habits** switch beside the filter chips; the address is `#athletes/habits` (`routeOf()`), Training is `#athletes`, Back works. One row per person the chosen chip shows who logged a habit or court time in 7 days: Sleep, Steps, Protein, Water (average of the days logged, a 7-bar strip) clay under three met days, Other habits as `met/possible`, Court. Each head is a button; the default is Sleep, lowest first, a habit with nothing logged counts lowest and a habit that is off goes last; Court sorts highest first. The rest fold under *N more logged nothing in 7 days*.
- **Court time** is `public.activity_log`, read for the file's athlete (14 days) when the file opens and for everyone (7 days) when Habits first opens, once per page life, emptied by ↻ Refresh. A failed read is `LOAD_ERR.activity`, named in the banner; the file draws no Court row and Habits says `n/a`, never "none".
- **Steps are the number that counts.** What they typed plus the Court Log's step credit (stage121; `moveCreditOfC()`, a copy of program.html's `moveCreditOf()` and the server's `move_steps_credit()`, held equal in `scripts/check_twins.js`), from 2026-11-22 on; the met, under, averages and strips all read it, and a credited cell's title says "2,000 walked + 7,000 from Tennis". If the court read failed, Steps are the typed number and the banner says why. `habCfgOfC()` is a copy of `habLoad()`'s config merge, also held there.
- **Tests.** `habgrid-file`, `habgrid-court-fails`, `habits-view`, `habits-view-court-fails`, `habgrid-demo` (fixtures: `buildSeed({ habitGrid: true })`); `design-rules` also measures `#athletes/habits`.

**Their own Steps number (stage125, merged the same day).** Steps in the grid and in Athletes → Habits is met at the athlete's own target on and after the day Amir set it (`steps_targets`, read once with the lists by `loadStepsTgtC()`, reloaded when a new number is set), and the activity credit is capped at 70% of that number, as `stepsScored()` and `hab_log_credited()` score it. A failed read is named in the banner and the days fall back to everyone's 10,000.
