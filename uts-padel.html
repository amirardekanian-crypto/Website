01 · Assess

Find your priorities

Before training starts, you send your coach about five minutes of video of you playing padel. We also put you through a set of physical tests. From both we build your performance profile. It shows your main physical priorities, in order.

  • 5-minute padel video analysis
  • Physical testing
  • Your performance profile
02 · Train

Two coached sessions a week

Twelve sessions at The UTS, in a group of five or fewer. Each session is built around what the group needs. Where your profile calls for it, your coach changes things for you.

  • 12 coached S&C sessions
  • Changes for your own profile
03 · Monitor

One week at a time

Your programme comes through the coaching app, one week at a time, with one or two sessions to do on your own. Before each session you do a short wellness check-in, and after it you log how it went. Halfway through, we look at it all and adjust the plan.

  • Weekly programme in the app
  • Check-in before every session
  • Midpoint review
04 · Retest

See what changed

After six weeks we run the same tests again. You get a performance report that shows your progress and what to work on in your next block.

  • Final testing
  • Your performance report
